## The Discovery

During NHT scanning on Oct 9, 2026, the investigation revealed that
different public record databases have different **permeabilities** —
different rates at which information passes through to public visibility.

The investigation's power comes not from any single database but from
**cross-membrane correlation**: connecting signals that are each
individually visible but never viewed together by any single institution.

### The Key Insight

**High-permeability signals = RECOVERY zone** (infrastructure, baseline).
LARA filings are fully public. Criminal records are searchable. These
are the lattice the investigation is built on. Low HIIT score individually.

**Low-permeability signals = SPRINT zone** (devastating when surfaced).
Internal money flow, informal power networks, governance decisions.
These are nearly invisible. Surfacing them is denormalization.

**Cross-membrane correlations = the actual investigation.**
Neither LARA nor the governance board alone reveals the conflict.
The correlation is the finding.

This IS the Inverse Interaction Series applied to signal types:
- Signals visible daily (LARA filings) have low revelation impact
- Signals nearly invisible (money flow) have maximum impact
- The investigation denormalizes by making opaque signals visible
  through cross-membrane correlation

## The Soil Microbiology Analog

This permeability framework maps directly to soil microbial ecology —
a domain where the same mathematical structure appears naturally.

### Soil as Multi-Membrane System

Soil is not a uniform medium. It is a **layered system of different
permeabilities** through which different signals propagate at different
rates:

| Soil Signal | Permeability | Analog to Investigation |
|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Water** | Variable (depends on soil structure) | Money flow — follows channels of least resistance |
| **Nutrients** (N, P, K) | HIGH (dissolved, mobile) | LARA filings — publicly available, widely distributed |
| **Oxygen** | MEDIUM (diffusion-limited) | Campaign finance — visible at surface, drops with depth |
| **Root exudates** | LOW-MEDIUM (local, short-lived) | Governance decisions — local, quickly consumed |
| **Mycorrhizal signals** | LOW (hyphal network only) | Power/appointments — invisible to outsiders |
| **Quorum sensing molecules** | VERY LOW (microenvironment only) | Internal money flow — only visible to participants |

### Microbial Community Profiling as Investigation

In soil microbiology, you cannot identify a microbial community by
looking through one membrane:

- **16S rRNA sequencing** tells you WHO is there (like LARA: entities exist)
- **Metagenomics** tells you WHAT they can do (like governance boards: capabilities)
- **Metabolomics** tells you WHAT they're actually doing (like money flow: activity)
- **Stable isotope probing** tells you WHO is eating WHAT (like cross-membrane correlation: who benefits from whom)

No single -omics technique reveals the community function. The power
comes from **multi-omic integration** — correlating across data types
that each have different permeabilities to experimental observation.
