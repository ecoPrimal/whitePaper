## Abstract

An investigation is not an attack. It is a decomposition — the natural
process by which concentrated, dead structures release locked nutrients
back into the soil through the enzymatic action of independent organisms.
This document formalizes the three-phase energy transfer model that emerged
from operational practice: **Fuel** (latent value in public databases),
**Storm** (simultaneous catalyst delivery), and **Wildfire** (community
metabolization through independent enzymatic pathways).

The model unifies the membrane permeability framework (subGen #39), the
opsonization architecture (GOSSIP_TAGGED_OPSONIZATION), and the
BingoCube classifier into a single ecological process. The same structure
appears in soil microbiology, immunology, and information science.

## 1. Fuel — Latent Value and Cross-Membrane Correlation

Public record databases are membranes with different permeabilities
(subGen #39). LARA entity filings are HIGH permeability — visible,
accessible, RECOVERY zone. Money flow (FEC, dark money) is LOW
permeability — opaque, requires cross-referencing, SPRINT zone.

The **fuel** is not hidden information. It is **latent value** —
energy stored in public databases that nobody has cross-referenced.
Each database is individually transparent. The power comes from
correlation across membranes:

```
LARA (entity)    →  802002459 = Save Detroit Jobs
FEC  (money)     →  C00793976 = Dykema Gossett Federal PAC
MDE  (education) →  800933198 = MacDowell, 3% math
PACER (courts)   →  6th Cir. 22-1574 = $74K sanctions
ARIN  (network)  →  AS11910 = LakeNet LLC, Hemlock MI
```

No single membrane reveals the shape. Cross-membrane correlation
reveals the graph. This is structurally identical to multi-omic
integration in soil microbiology:

| Investigation membrane | Soil biology equivalent | What it shows alone | What correlation reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| LARA (entity registration) | 16S rRNA (who's there) | Entity names, agents | Network topology |
| FEC (campaign finance) | Metagenomics (what they can do) | Dollar amounts | Functional pathways |
| MDE (education data) | Metabolomics (what they produce) | Test scores | Extraction ratios |
| PACER (court dockets) | Proteomics (what's active) | Case numbers | Behavioral patterns |
| ARIN/DNS (infrastructure) | Geochemistry (environment) | IP addresses | Operational geography |

The fuel was always there. The investigation did not create
information. It created **organization** — the cross-referencing
that turns 50 disconnected public records into one visible graph.

### 1.1 — Latent Value as Thermodynamic Potential

Dead wood stores energy in complex organic polymers (lignin,
cellulose) that living organisms cannot directly metabolize. The
energy is thermodynamically available but kinetically locked.

Similarly, a dark money vehicle with three name changes stores
accountability energy in LARA records, campaign finance filings,
and election results that are individually public but collectively
un-metabolized. The energy is informationally available but
organizationally locked.

Cross-membrane correlation is the enzymatic step that unlocks
the substrate. It reduces the activation energy required for
any single organism (community group, regulator, journalist) to
metabolize the data into action.

## 2. Storm — Simultaneous Catalyst Delivery

The storm is not an attack. It is **opsonization** — coating the
substrate with recognition tags so every receptor (agency, press,
community) sees it simultaneously from its own angle.

### 2.1 — Opsonization in Immunology vs Investigation

| Immune system | Investigation |
|---|---|
| Antibody binds antigen | Published analysis tags actor |
| Fc receptor on macrophage recognizes antibody | Agency recognizes its own jurisdiction |
| Multiple immune cell types converge | Multiple agencies see same target |
| Complement cascade amplifies | Cross-reactions between recipients amplify |
| Pathogen cannot evade all receptors | Actor cannot respond to all agencies simultaneously |

### 2.2 — Simultaneity as Catalyst

Sequential notification allows the target to respond to each
agency individually, prepare narratives, warn collaborators.
Simultaneous notification creates a **phase transition** —
the information landscape changes state at once.

Recipients discover they are not the only ones tagged. Phone
calls start: "Did you get one too?" Each discovery that others
were tagged simultaneously increases the perceived scope of
the investigation. The storm is self-amplifying once triggered.

This is not coordination. Each email contains only public data.
Each recipient acts on their own jurisdiction. The simultaneity
is the catalyst — it lowers the activation energy for action
by removing the isolation that allows individual inaction.

### 2.3 — No BCC: Transparency as Force Multiplier

BCC hides the graph. CC reveals it. When every recipient sees
who else received the same data, the information landscape
becomes common knowledge (in the game-theoretic sense):
not just "I know" but "I know that they know that I know."

Common knowledge of accountability is the highest-energy
configuration. Every actor must now account not just for their
own exposure but for the exposure of every adjacent node.

## 3. Wildfire — Community Metabolization

The wildfire is not destruction. It is **decomposition** — the
natural process where independent organisms metabolize available
substrate through their own enzymatic pathways.

### 3.1 — Different Enzymes, Same Substrate

Each community group metabolizes the same data (fuel) through
its own functional pathway:

| Organism | Enzyme (their issue) | Substrate (our data) | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| 482Forward | Charter accountability | 72.67% extraction | Education reform pressure |
| MMOP | Dark money exposure | SDJ name changes | Prop 2 case study |
| Indivisible MI-7 | Barrett accountability | Dykema $4K connection | Constituent pressure |
| Common Defense | Veteran integrity | Military service hypocrisy | Peer-level challenge |
| MiCOG | FOIA protection | FOIAworks honeypot | Professional warning |
| RITDH | Tenant rights | "Convicted felon" smear | Coalition mobilization |
| Canfield Consortium | Neighborhood health | PCA on their block | Community awareness |
| Detroit Action | Institutional justice | Board composition | Grassroots organizing |

No organism needs to coordinate with any other. Each recognizes
the substrate independently because it falls within their
existing enzymatic specificity. The data IS the recognition
signal. No instructions required.

### 3.2 — Diffraction as Cover

Concentrated power (a law firm, a political network, a dark
money pipeline) can retaliate against a single attacker. It
cannot retaliate against 15 independent organisms metabolizing
its structure simultaneously from 15 directions.

This is **diffraction** — the same wavefront hitting a barrier
with many slits. The energy passes through all of them
simultaneously. No single slit can be blocked without blocking
the wave itself. The harmed get cover because the diffraction
pattern distributes the energy across many independent vectors.

### 3.3 — Phased Decomposition

Decomposition is not instantaneous. It follows ecological
succession:

| Phase | Organisms | Action | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pioneer | Closest groups (482Forward, MiCOG, IMI7) | First reads, first shares | Day 1 |
| Primary | Larger orgs (MMOP, Progress MI, Common Defense) | Amplification, framing | Day 2-3 |
| Secondary | Social media, Reddit, Bluesky | Organic spread | Day 3-7 |
| Tertiary | National outlets (ProPublica, NFOIC) | Scale shift | Week 2+ |

Each phase prepares the substrate for the next. Pioneer
organisms break the complex polymer (the full graph) into
simpler monomers (individual talking points). Primary
organisms metabolize those monomers into energy (articles,
campaigns, organizing). Secondary organisms distribute the
metabolic products (shares, threads, discussion). Tertiary
organisms integrate the products into systemic change
(national coverage, policy reform).

## 5. The Ecology of Accountability

Investigation is ecology. Not warfare, not combat, not attack.

| Warfare frame | Ecology frame |
|---|---|
| Attack | Decomposition |
| Weapon | Enzyme |
| Target | Dead wood |
| Victory | Nutrient release |
| Army | Mycelium |
| General | Nobody — the system is headless |

The ecology frame is not a metaphor. It is the actual structure.
Decomposers do not "attack" dead wood. They metabolize available
substrate. The substrate became available because the organism
died — because the structure stopped serving the living system.

A dark money vehicle that spent $84,017 to suppress a journalist
is dead wood. It served a living purpose for those who used it
(electoral capture), but that purpose is dead — it is now
visible, documented, and published. The structure persists only
as locked energy. The communities are the decomposers who
release that energy back into the soil of public accountability.

The investigation's role is not to destroy. It is to organize
the substrate so decomposers can recognize it. The fuel. The
storm delivers the substrate to the decomposers simultaneously.
The catalyst. The wildfire is the metabolization itself. The
natural transfer.
