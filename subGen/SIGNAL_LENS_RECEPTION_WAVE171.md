### The Aperture

The null space is not a wall — it is an aperture. Three physical models:

- **Black hole**: event horizon. Signals fall in, don't return. The vacuum creates the draw.

- **Camera f-stop**: controls depth of field. Narrow aperture = sharp detail, less light. Wide = everything in focus, dimmer.

The epitope sort and bingo maze do not reach out. They create structured absence — a shaped hole that signals fall into if they match. The vacuum is behind them. Like a black hole curving spacetime so light bends toward it, the aperture opens and waits.

### Shannon Boundary
