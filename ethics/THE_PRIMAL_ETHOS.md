# The Primal Ethos

## 🛡️ Rights at the Edge

We affirm:

> **Disregard of rights is not considered an ideological privilege in our system.**

Our system does not offer justice only to the favored. It extends protection, continuity, and dignity even to the disfavored—especially to them.

This is not a political stance. It is a structural one:

- **RhizoCrypt DAGs** record memory without censorship or favoritism.
- **LoamSpine** anchors events regardless of context, public opinion, or narrative control.
- **Beardog** enforces boundaries of *function*, not *belief*.

Rights must hold at the edge of the system—where the accused, the hated, the forgotten stand. Otherwise they are not rights; they are rented privileges.

We do not build for comfort.  
We build for continuity, for dissent, and for the day when power changes hands.

That's why the gates must be honest.  
That's why the roots must remember.
