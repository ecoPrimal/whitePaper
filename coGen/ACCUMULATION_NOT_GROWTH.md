## The Rotation

Growing silicon is the Czochralski process — 1,425°C, argon atmosphere, ultra-pure
substrate, billions of dollars per fab. Pressure making diamonds. The result is
extraordinary but static. It does not grow. It does not learn. It does not improve
with seasons.

A coral reef is accumulated. Billions of organisms, each depositing a layer, each
layer compressed by the ones above it. No clean rooms. Just time and a selectively
permeable membrane. The reef scales with time. The fab scales with energy.

The entire AI industry is making the diamond bet: more pressure → more compute →
better results. The ecoPrimal architecture makes the reef bet: more seasons → more
compressed residue → better pattern.

Loamspine is literally named for this. Loam = accumulated organic matter. The ledger
grows by not forgetting.
