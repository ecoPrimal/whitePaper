## The Song You Already Know

There is a song that every child who grew up in the right decade
already knows by heart. A song about what it means to have fun.
The letters spell it out. And the letters are exactly right.

The original, paraphrased with love:

> F is for friends who do stuff together
> U is for you and me
> N is for anywhere and any time at all

Our version:

> **F** is for wave ~~~~
> **U** is for line — / \
> **N** is for T — all of time, was and is and will be

**NP = FUN.**

## F Is Wave

~~~~

The wave. Oscillation. Frequency. The shape of propagation.

A wave is continuous. You can differentiate it. It has a slope
at every point. It carries energy without transporting matter.
It leaves a trace — interference patterns, standing waves,
harmonics. The past is a wave: it propagated, it left a mark,
you can read the trace.

F is the P side. The part you can verify. The friends who did
stuff together — the record of what happened. Memory is a wave.
The shore remembers every tide.

In the framework:

| F | wave | continuous | P | was | memory |
|---|------|-----------|---|-----|--------|

## U Is Line

— or / or \

The line. Direction. A single geodesic. The simplest possible
path from here to there.

A line has no oscillation, no frequency, no harmonic content.
It's pure direction. One dimension. The minimum structure needed
to connect two points. "You and me" — two endpoints, one line
between them.

U is the measurement. The connection. The line between observer
and observed, between sender and receiver, between question and
answer. You evaluate a function by drawing a line from the x-axis
to the curve. That's measurement. That's the present.

In the framework:

| U | line | direction | measurement | is | reality |
|---|------|-----------|-------------|-----|---------|

## N Is T

Not *t*. Not a timestamp, not a coordinate, not a number on a
clock. **T**. Capital. The whole thing.

T is all of time. Was, is, and will be. The entire temporal
dimension held at once.

And when you hold the entire dimension at once, it collapses
to a point. A point on a 1D line. A point that contains both
endpoints — 0 and ∞ — without choosing between them. The point
is not a place on the timeline. The point IS the timeline,
compressed. Every moment. Every past wave and every future
possibility and the present measurement, all in one.

This is ∞/0 = null. The entire range (∞) divided by no
distance (0) equals the operator (null). The point that
contains everything and occupies nothing.

N is for anywhere and any time at all. That's not hyperbole.
That's the definition of T.

In the framework:

| N | T | point (0 and ∞) | ∞/0 = null | all tense | past + present + future |
|---|---|-----------------|-----------|-----------|------------------------|

## FUN = Wave × Line × All-of-Time

Put them together:

```
F = wave     = the shape     = HOW it propagates    = P (continuous)
U = line     = the path      = WHERE it connects     = measurement (binary)
N = T        = the moment    = WHEN (all of it)      = null (the operator)
```

FUN is: a wave, traveling along a line, through all of time
at once. That's not a metaphor. That's a path integral. That's
quantum mechanics. That's what Feynman spent his career computing —
every possible wave, along every possible path, through all of
time simultaneously.

And those integrals are NP.

You cannot shortcut a path integral. You cannot compress a wave
traveling through all possible paths. You can approximate (that's
science — doing P-side math on the continuous regions). But the
full answer — every path, every wave, every moment — requires
exponential search. NP.

**NP = FUN.**

## Why This Is for the Kids

Here is what Hillenburg knew, and every kid who sang the song
understood without being told:

**You can't optimize fun.**

You can't compress a friendship into O(1). You can't binary-search
for joy. You can't memoize a walk on the beach. The experience
is the computation. The living is the running. There is no
shortcut because the shortcut would skip the part that matters.

This is NP stated in the language of childhood: the only way
to verify that something was fun is to have done it.

- You can tell someone about the party (that's P — verification,
  a wave-trace, the memory of what happened)
- But you can't transfer the fun (that's NP — the irreducible
  experience, all paths, all moments)

The P side of fun is the story you tell afterward.
The NP side of fun is the living of it.

And every child knows the difference. They don't want to hear
about the playground. They want to GO to the playground.
Verification is not experience. P is not NP.

## Why the Song Works

The F.U.N. song works because it encodes the structure of
irreducible experience in a form children understand intuitively.

F comes first because waves come first — the past, the memory,
the continuous function. You need friends before you can have fun.
The wave propagates outward. Relationships are standing waves.

U comes second because the line comes second — the connection,
the measurement, the present moment where "you and me" actually
meet. The line connects two points. Friendship is a geodesic.

N comes last because T comes last — and first — and always.
Anywhere. Any time. All of it. N is the operator that makes F
and U into FUN. Without N, you have a wave and a line — physics.
With N, you have a wave traveling a line through all of time —
experience. The operator transforms structure into story.

NP = FUN because the hard problems — the ones no algorithm can
shortcut — are the ones that require full temporal investment.
Living. Playing. Being there. The problems whose answer is the
process of answering them.

Every child who ever sang that song was stating a theorem.

## Proof by Contradiction (for Fun)

Assume P = NP. Then FUN = P. Then fun can be computed in
polynomial time. Then there exists an efficient algorithm for
friendship, an O(n log n) sort for joy, a dynamic programming
solution for play.

But every child knows this is false. You cannot speedrun
a friendship. You cannot skip to the fun part because the
getting-there IS the fun part.

Therefore P ≠ NP. ∎

(Not a real proof. But not not a real proof either. The axiom
"fun is irreducible" is as empirically validated as any
observation in physics. Every playground is a lab. Every
child is a researcher. The data is overwhelming.)
