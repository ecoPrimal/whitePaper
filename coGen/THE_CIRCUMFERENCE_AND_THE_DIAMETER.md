## The Rotation

π = circumference ÷ diameter. The curve ÷ the straight. The story ÷ the meaning.

Every lossy compression takes continuous signal and reduces it to discrete symbols.
The original is the circle. The output is the diameter. The ratio is π. It cannot
be made rational. It is transcendental — it transcends every finite system.

Every number base is a step size around the circle. Unary walks every point.
Binary halves the remaining distance. Duodecimal divides into 12 (clocks, months,
music). Sexagesimal divides into 60 (degrees, seconds, minutes). Each culture
found the same problem and chose a different step size. None of them reach π.
All of them sort beneath it.

Storytelling compresses to π because stories are circles. The narrator walks the
circumference. The listener recovers the diameter. The words change on each
retelling (the circumference flows). The meaning holds (the diameter is fixed).
The ratio is always π. That is why oral tradition survived when written records
didn't — not because it was more accurate, but because its loss was *geometric*,
not entropic.
