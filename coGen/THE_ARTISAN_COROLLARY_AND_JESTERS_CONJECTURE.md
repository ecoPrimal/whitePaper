# PART I: THE ARTISAN'S COROLLARY

## The Formal Consequence

If the anti-secret is true — if publishing everything achieves zero-knowledge
by making the information universal — then the corollary is:

> **The size of the public record IS the size of the privacy.**

This is the Artisan's Corollary.

## The Proof

250+ documents. 103 sporePrint science papers. 43 coGen entries. 16
hypotheses. 6 philosophy essays. 3 kAnGen inoculations. 2 gAIa papers.
Every single one is public. Every single one is on GitHub, rendered on
sporePrint, published on hypothesis.primals.eco. Not one is behind a login.

And yet: nobody knows what comes next.

Nobody can predict Paper 88 from Papers 1–87. Nobody can infer the next
coGen entry from the previous 43. The corpus is 250+ published clock
towers, and the 251st is zero-knowledge — not because it's hidden, but
because the space of possible next-papers is infinite.

This is a real property of the system:

| Corpus size | Public papers | Hidden papers | Privacy |
|------------|--------------|--------------|---------|
| 1 | 1 | 0 | Low — the next paper is somewhat predictable |
| 10 | 10 | 0 | Medium — branching factor grows |
| 100 | 100 | 0 | High — too many threads to follow |
| 250 | 250 | 0 | Complete — nobody can see the whole graph |

The privacy grows with the record. Each paper ADDS privacy by expanding
the search space. Not by hiding — by publishing. The 250th paper makes
the 251st harder to predict, not easier.

This is the opposite of surveillance capitalism. In surveillance: more
data = less privacy (they know you better). In anti-secret: more data =
more privacy (they know less about what comes next, because the graph has
too many edges to traverse).

## Formal Statement

Let **P** = the set of all published documents.
Let **N** = the next document (unknown).
Let **H(N|P)** = the conditional entropy of the next document given all published ones.

**Corollary**: H(N|P) is non-decreasing in |P|.

As the corpus grows, the entropy of the next paper does not decrease. It
may increase. Each new paper opens new threads, new connections, new
possible next-steps. The more you publish, the less predictable you become.

This is because the graph is creative, not deductive. Deductive corpora
narrow (axioms → theorems → proofs, the space shrinks). Creative corpora
branch (papers → connections → new questions, the space grows).

The ecoPrimal corpus is creative. The anti-secret works because the corpus
is not narrowing toward a conclusion. It is branching toward more questions.
kAnGen is the name for this: the question IS the tool. More questions = more
privacy = more freedom.

H(N|P) ≥ H(N|P∖{p}) for creative corpora.

Every paper you remove REDUCES the entropy. Every paper you add INCREASES it.
Publishing is privacy. The Artisan's Corollary. ∎

# PART II: THE JESTER'S CONJECTURE

## Science Is a Little Goofy

Let me tell you what happened.

A microbiologist in Michigan was looking at his VPS. He had 12 services.
6 were alive. 6 were dead. He said: "12 is over-stable. It divides too
cleanly. We need it to be 13."

So he wrote a script that ate the dead services and tagged their
successors. That was service 13. The sourdough-cache. It lived in the
irrational remainder of 4π.

Then he said: "huh, 12 chimes into 1 is 13."

And that is how a man with a dead Forgejo instance discovered why the
Babylonians chose 12-hour timekeeping 4,000 years ago.

## The Conjecture

**The Jester's Conjecture: If your scientific finding makes you laugh,
you're probably right.**

Not "correct." *Right.* Correct is P-side — verifiable, checkable, the
math works. Right is NP-side — you know it when you feel it, you can't
prove the feeling, the witness is the laugh.

NP = FUN (coGen 12). You can't optimize fun. You can't shortcut friendship.
The only way to verify that something was fun is to have done it. The only
way to verify that something is true in the deep sense is that it surprises
you into laughing.

Evidence:

| Discovery | What happened | Funny? |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| 12 → 13 via chime | Counting VPS services → ancient timekeeping | Yes |
| The anti-secret | "What if we hide nothing?" → same privacy as hiding everything | Absurdly yes |
