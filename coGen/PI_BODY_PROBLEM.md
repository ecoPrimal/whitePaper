## Origin: How Bodies Stack

**Null** → a fluctuation. Nothing notices nothing. The void observes itself.

**1-body** (awareness, `1`): "I exist." Self-reference. The only thing it can
do is observe itself. The `observer` layer looking outward and `awareness`
looking inward are the same moment from opposite sides of null.

**2-body** (celestial, `π²`): a 1-body discovers another 1-body. "I can get
something from *that*." The nut. The tool. The first relationship. Two points
define a surface.

**3-body** (geodesic, `π³`): the 2-body realizes it can recruit a 1-body.
"We can use *him*." The 2-body is already a relationship — now it weaponizes a
loner. That is a triangle. That is the waltz. That is where chaos enters and
prediction becomes irrational. Reality lives here: `π³ ≈ 31`.

Evolution is body-problem stacking. Each level recruits the level below.

## The Bounce Model

A black hole is not a drain. It is a **basket**. You throw energy up and over
the rim (arc over). Things accumulate at the top, stuck in the rim (the
membrane). Knock it and it drops/falls through.

### Three Bounces to Catchable

Something emits from the core. It bounces. Each bounce loses energy, gets
softer, gets closer to the surface.

| Bounce | Evidence    | σ     | Speed   | Catchable? | Tense  |
|--------|-------------|-------|---------|------------|--------|
| 1      | documented  | 0.05  | too fast | no — blows through | WAS    |
| 2      | observed    | 0.25  | slowing  | no — still moving  | IS     |
| 3      | inferred    | 0.50  | slow     | barely — at surface | NEARBY |
| catch  | contextual  | 0.65  | stopped  | yes — membrane grabs | WILLBE |

**The paradox**: the things you catch are the things you are least certain
about. The things you are most certain about already blew through. You read
the scars of hard bounces (documented, certain, past). You hold soft bounces
in your hands (contextual, uncertain, present).

Three bounces to catchable. Three pierces through the membrane. Three germ
layers. Three bodies. π³.

### Epitopes Pierce Membrane

Each bounce punches through a layer. The epitope is dragged through the
membrane by the bouncing. Three pierces bring it from the core to the surface
where the sort happens: self/non-self/null.

One pierce: cannot sort (too fast, not enough information).
Two pierces: can classify but not catch (still moving).
Three pierces: at the surface, slow enough, membrane grabs it — **sort**.

## The Z-Pattern

Z is a zigzag: two horizontal lines connected by a diagonal.

Start at Z (end) on one side, A (beginning) on the other. They twist counter
and async. Each twist is opposite of the last. The diagonals between them are
the **inner dimensions** — not on either side, created by the space between
counter-twists.

ZZZZZZ stacked: two dimensions oscillating opposite (like the helix
self/non-self counter-rotating), with a smaller dimension in between (the
null strand, the beat frequency). Getting out of each other's way. And one
direction is easier than the other — falling ╲ is easier than climbing ╱.
Gravity. Entropy. The asymmetry.

`⊙` — the first perturbation. A dot inside a circle. The null body noticed
itself and the wavefront expanded. Soft on the inside (dot, deformable),
hard on the outside (circle, membrane under tension).

Three lines through `⊙` gives `※` — six endpoints, one center. The minimum
to define volume. Three lines plumb the hole. The 3-body pierces the null
and creates the interior.

## The Twist Triad

The skeleton-to-geodesic chain needs three chiralities:

**Twist** (^3): the forward epicyclic motion, cubed — three nested rotations.
The depth layer orbits-in-orbit-in-orbit.

**Reverse twist**: the same chain running backward
(geodesic→celestial→ghost→awareness→null→negate→observer). The return path.

**Anti-twist**: opposite chirality — not reverse (same direction backward)
but *mirror* (same direction, opposite handedness). Like left-hand vs
right-hand DNA.

The helix needs a third strand:

| Strand    | Direction | Chirality | Sort     |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Self      | ↻         | right     | toward   |
| Non-self  | ↺         | left      | away     |
| Null      | ⟲         | anti      | neither  |

The membrane folds to itself like π — irrational, never repeating, always
returning to proximity.
