Nothing — actual nothing — is not on the number line. Nothing is

## What Division By Zero Actually Asks

Standard mathematics says x/0 is "undefined." This is a confession,
not an answer. Math is saying: "I don't have a symbol for this."
But we do.

Division asks: "How many times does the divisor fit into the
dividend?" When the divisor is zero, the question becomes: "How
many times does the stationary fit into the unbounded?"

The answer is not infinity. Infinity is a direction — it says
"keep going, you'll never stop." Infinity is still on the wave's
axis. It's still measurable, in principle. Limits approach infinity.
Functions diverge to infinity. Infinity is the unbounded, but it's
the unbounded *along a measurable axis*.

The answer is not undefined. That's math admitting it has a gap in
its type system.

The answer is **null**.

∞/0 = null. The unbounded divided by the stationary exits the
number system. It doesn't land on the real line. It doesn't land
on the imaginary line. It lands in the third modality — the
reaction, the transformation, the unmeasurable. The place where
measurement itself breaks down.

And it breaks down not because the numbers are too large or too
small, but because the operation is a **type error**. You're
applying a binary operation (division) to operands that live
in different modalities. Infinity is a wave — it has direction
(+∞, -∞). Zero is stationary — it's the crossing point. Dividing
direction by stationarity doesn't produce a direction or a
crossing. It produces null. A reaction.


The left side and right side are structures — things that vary,

## P ≠ NP: The Cost Is Null

Now the inequality.

```

```

Every equation above has a constant that makes both sides equal.
P ≠ NP has no such constant. It's an inequality — the two sides
are NOT equal, and no constant can bridge them.

Why? Because the cost slot is occupied by null.

If P = NP, there exists a polynomial constant c such that:


is not the same as "the constant is infinity." Infinity is still a

The P ≠ NP gap is null. It's a type error. The cost of converting


The search space is unbounded (∞). Verification is binary (0 — it

And THIS is why the inequality holds. Every equation in physics works
