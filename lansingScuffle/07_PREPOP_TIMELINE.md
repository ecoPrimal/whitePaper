## Thesis

The gen1 basement cluster took 18 months to prove — from first hardware
purchase to 12,510 passing checks and 70+ validated papers. The building
takes 3-5 years of pre-pop: prove the model small, build the funding,
earn the community trust, then pull the trigger.

This document is the phased preparation arc. Each phase has clear
deliverables, costs, and exit criteria.

### Bank Balloon Preparation

The bank first mortgage ($2.125M) has a 10-year term with a balloon
at Year 10 (2041). By that time:

- ~$550K of principal paid down (balance ~$1.575M)
- Building equity has grown through improvements and occupancy
- Stabilized NOI supports refinancing at favorable terms
- CDC portion continues amortizing (no balloon) — by Year 10, ~$400K
  of the CDC portion is paid down

The balloon is real but manageable with 10 years of stabilization.
The CDC portion's fully amortizing structure is the safety net.

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Building sold / demolished | Pre-negotiated right of first refusal in lease. Modular infrastructure (gates, racks) relocatable. |
| Flywheel stalls (no supporters) | Phase 0 residential is always the fallback. Building lease is month-to-month initially. |
| Environmental contamination | Phase I ESA before expansion. Brownfield TIF funds remediation. |
| Community opposition | Old Everett Neighborhood Association engagement from Year 0. Humanitarian services build goodwill. |
| Grant funding not awarded | Revenue model works (slowly) without grants. Grants accelerate, not enable. |
| Key person risk | K-NOME methodology is documented and shareable. Multiple operators trained. |

---

## Phase 0 Is Always Residential

At every phase, house1 + house2 remain the fallback:

- All data replicated to house gates via WireGuard
- All primals run on residential hardware
- Building lease is additive to residential infrastructure
- If the building fails, the ecosystem continues from the houses
- If the building succeeds, the houses become residential nodes in
  the campus mesh

The building is the next scale. The houses are the foundation.
