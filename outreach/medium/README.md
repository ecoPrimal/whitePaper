### Article 4 Series Note

The "Manifestos" series is five pieces that can be read independently or as
a sequence. The main article (04) is the structural juxtaposition. The
companions go deeper: 04a into the human cost and preconditions, 04b into
the tollbooth architecture of the modern economy, 04c into the kingdom
pattern across individuals and institutions, 04d into the specific betrayal
of Detachment 201 — tech executives commissioned as Lieutenant Colonels
with no service, no qualification, and no recusal from billion-dollar
contracts.

Reception determines which get published first — 04 is the sharpest, 04d
is the most topical and infuriating, 04b is the most conceptually novel,
04a is the most emotionally grounded, 04c is the broadest.
