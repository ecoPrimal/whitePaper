## The Three Registers

There are many selves within one person. A surgeon is both the detachment
of the scalpel and the attunement of the poet. These are not masks — they
are modes of attention. The separation is tonal, not secretive. The
audience is invited to read both.

The framing is trinitarian — not to claim power, but to claim humility.
If one of me is not enough, if parts of me will be too weak or too
shallow, the other parts carry the weight. ecoPrimal alone is just
numbers. attsi alone is just anger. Tamison alone is just curiosity.
Three persons, one substance — not because any claims divinity but
because each admits insufficiency. The builder without the humanist is
shallow. The humanist without the builder is weightless. The scientist
without both is silent. The trust between them is: I know I am not
enough, and I know you carry what I cannot.

But the trust does more than comfort. It liberates. Each register can
go further because the others hold the ground.

Tamison can be more curious — can wander further into the strange, the
speculative, the "what if Anderson localization governs biofilm
signaling" — because ecoPrimal has the systems validated and attsi has
already mapped the long-term impacts. The curiosity is safe to roam
because the infrastructure is load-bearing and the ethical perimeter is
drawn. I can explore more freely if I know the long consequences ahead
of time.

ecoPrimal can take bigger technical risks — try the DF64 shader on
untested hardware, push the lattice QCD to larger volumes, add a 14th
service — because attsi has already articulated what matters and what
is a tollbooth. The builder knows which risks are worth taking because
the humanist has mapped the territory.

attsi can push the critique harder — name the contempt, describe the
Sackler architecture, call out Detachment 201 — because ecoPrimal has
the running system that makes the argument non-hypothetical. The anger
is not performative because the alternative is live.

Each expects it from the others. Not hopes — expects. The trust is
structural, not sentimental. ecoPrimal expects attsi to see the cost
and name it. attsi expects ecoPrimal to carry the weight and prove it.
Both expect Tamison to keep asking the questions that started
everything — because without the curiosity, there is nothing to build
and nothing to defend.

### ecoPrimal

**Register**: The builder. The reproducible math. The proof.

ecoPrimal speaks in specifics. Test counts, hardware specs, deploy paths,
provenance chains, dependency inventories. ecoPrimal shows the work. When
ecoPrimal makes a claim, the evidence follows in the same sentence. The
tone is pragmatic, precise, and lets the numbers do the arguing. ecoPrimal
does not editorialize about what the numbers mean for civilization — that
is attsi's domain. ecoPrimal says: "here is what we built, here is the
evidence, here is how to verify it."

**Publishes**: Technical articles, builder narratives, Reddit posts,
hardware documentation, science validation reports.

**Platform**: Medium (ecoPrimal account), Reddit (ecoPrimal handle),
dev.to (cross-post), GitHub (ecoPrimals org).

**Tone markers**: Concrete nouns. Specific numbers. Code blocks. Tables.
"We built." "The test suite shows." "You can verify." Minimal adjectives.
No rhetorical questions.

**Trust**: ecoPrimal trusts attsi to see the *why* behind the cost —
the reason the basement exists instead of a pitch deck, the reason the
electricity comes out of one person's pocket. ecoPrimal cannot articulate
this; the numbers are silent about their own meaning. attsi sees what
ecoPrimal carries and names it.

**Expectation**: ecoPrimal expects attsi to have already drawn the
ethical perimeter. When ecoPrimal decides to bypass CUDA and build on
Vulkan, ecoPrimal expects that attsi has already articulated why vendor
lock-in is a tollbooth — so the technical decision is not just
pragmatic but structurally sound. ecoPrimal takes bigger risks because
the map of what matters has already been drawn.

### attsi

**Register**: The humanist. The structural critic. The disgust.

attsi speaks in structures. Preconditions, tollbooths, kingdoms,
architecture of contempt, who gets to look down on whom. attsi names the
anger and disciplines it into argument. The tone is cold, precise, and
architectural — the fury is real but channeled through structural analysis
rather than personal attack. attsi does not name individuals to condemn
them — attsi describes the pattern so clearly that everyone recognizes it,
including in themselves.

When attsi cites technical specifics, it is as evidence that the
alternative *exists* — the point is not the number, it is that the
manifesto writers don't have one.

**Trust**: attsi trusts ecoPrimal to carry the weight of what attsi
describes. The wells are not metaphors — someone dug them. The sovereign
compute is not an argument — someone pays the electricity. attsi names
the architecture of contempt; ecoPrimal built the alternative that makes
the contempt visible by contrast. attsi's critique has weight because
ecoPrimal's system is running.

**Expectation**: attsi expects ecoPrimal to have the system live and
validated. When attsi names the Palantir manifesto as architectural
contempt, attsi expects that the counterexample is not hypothetical —
that 175 checks passed this week, that the JupyterHub is serving
researchers right now, that the provenance chain is unbroken. attsi
pushes harder because the proof is already in hand.

**Publishes**: Philosophical essays, structural critiques, ethical
analysis, the "While They Were Writing Manifestos" series.

**Platform**: Medium (attsi account), cross-post to dev.to.

**Tone markers**: Structural metaphors. Long sentences that build to a
turn. "The architecture of this is not subtle." "The disgust is not
personal — it is architectural." "The bread thief is downstream. The
precondition is upstream." Rhetorical questions that answer themselves.
Cold precision, not heat.

### Tamison

**Register**: The biological curiosity. The bench scientist. The person
who put his hands in the soil.

Tamison stays reserved. Not published in this series. Referenced obliquely
by both voices when the personal history is structurally necessary ("one
of the people who built this system served in the infantry," "the author's
background is bench microbiology"). Tamison is the human being behind the
registers. The audience may infer the connection but the articles do not
state it.

Tamison is the ground. The one who put gloves on a man's hands. The one
who cultured anaerobes at BSL2 and counted spores under a microscope. The
one who carried the radio and managed the crypto keys. ecoPrimal and attsi
both draw from Tamison's experience without speaking his name. He is the
substrate — the curiosity that started everything, the body that was in
the cold, the hands that were in the soil.

**Trust**: Tamison trusts ecoPrimal to keep the systems validated and
attsi to keep the ethical perimeter drawn. With that trust, Tamison can
be more curious — can follow the Anderson localization thread from
condensed matter to biofilms to immunology without worrying whether the
infrastructure will hold or whether the exploration will wander into a
tollbooth. The curiosity is safe to roam because the other two have the
long consequences mapped. I can explore more freely if I know the
weight is being carried.

**Expectation**: ecoPrimal and attsi both expect Tamison to keep asking
the questions that neither of them would think to ask. The question
that started everything — "does this math apply to biofilms?" — is a
Tamison question. Without it, there is nothing to build and nothing to
defend. The curiosity is not idle; it is the source.

**Publishes**: Nothing in this outreach series. Tamison is reserved for
ABG (the research community) and for faculty-level contact (Kevin Mok).
But Tamison's curiosity drives the science that ecoPrimal validates and
attsi contextualizes. The unpublished register is the generative one.

## Internal Framings (Not Published)

### The Surgeon
A surgeon can be both detached as his scalpel and as in tune as a poet
when it is needed. These are not different people. They are different
modes of the same person's attention, each suited to its task.

### The Trinity
Three persons, one substance. Not the Trinity of power — the Trinity of
humility. The acknowledgment that one register is not enough. The
Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit are not three gods claiming three
kinds of authority. They are one God admitting that the fullness of the
work requires more than one mode of presence.

ecoPrimal is the hands — the one who builds, who carries, who pays the
cost in silence. attsi is the voice — the one who names what the hands
cannot articulate, who sees the architecture and speaks the disgust.
Tamison is the ground — the curiosity, the body, the one who was in the
cold and in the soil. None is sufficient. Each carries what the others
cannot.

The choice to model this on the Trinity is not a claim of divinity. It
is the opposite. It is the admission that I am not enough. That the
builder without the humanist is shallow. That the humanist without the
builder is weightless. That both without the quiet scientist who
started it all are ungrounded. The structure is humility, not power.

But the Trinity is not only about shared burden. It is about liberation
through trust. The Son goes freely into the world because the Father
holds the ground and the Spirit carries the meaning. Each person of the
Trinity can go further — can take on more, risk more, give more —
because the others are holding what they cannot hold themselves.

Tamison can be more curious because the systems hold weight. ecoPrimal
can take bigger risks because the ethical map is drawn. attsi can push
harder because the proof is live. The trust is not just "I know you
carry what I cannot." It is "because you carry it, I can go further
than I could alone." The insufficiency of each is the freedom of each.

Ethnically Catholic. Philosophically deist. The Trinity is chosen not
for its theology but for its architecture: if one of me will be too
weak, the others carry the weight — and because they carry it, I am
free.

### Ender's Game
The Valentine/Demosthenes parallel applies structurally: two public
voices, one strategic mind. But unlike the Wiggin children, the
connection is not hidden. It is the point. And unlike the Wiggin
children, the relationship is not strategic manipulation — it is trust.
Each voice trusts the other to carry what it cannot.
