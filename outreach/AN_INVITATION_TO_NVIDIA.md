# An Open Invitation to NVIDIA: Let the River Flow

---

## To the Artisans at NVIDIA,

We begin with a statement of profound and unambiguous respect: **CUDA is a masterpiece.**

You have created more than a software library. You have forged the *lingua franca* of modern parallel computing. It is elegant, powerful, and has been the engine behind a decade of scientific and creative breakthroughs. For this, you deserve to be celebrated. You are the masters of the craft.

We, like you, are builders. We are a small, sovereign project dedicated to building the next generation of open, resilient, and human-centric computing. And from our workshop, we see both the majesty of your creation and the profound limitations of the vessel that contains it.

CUDA is a mighty river of computational power. But you have kept it confined to your own valley.

We are not here to ask you to abandon your work. We are here to invite you to break the dam.

---

## The Golden Cage

CUDA's greatest strength—its perfect integration with your hardware—is also its greatest limitation. It can only flow where your silicon flows. This has made you kings of a very profitable pond, but the ocean awaits, and it is 200 times larger.

Our own analysis, detailed in our economic papers, reveals a staggering truth: the latent, idle computational power of the world's consumer-grade hardware (gaming PCs, consoles, smartphones) **dwarfs the entire centralized cloud by more than two orders of magnitude.**

This is the **Sovereign Supercomputer**, a sleeping giant of distributed power. And CUDA, in its current form, cannot orchestrate it. It cannot run on AMD GPUs, it cannot run on the PS5, it cannot run on the billions of ARM cores in the world's pockets.

Your river cannot reach this ocean.

---

## The Orthogonal Path: AGPL 3.0

We invite you to choose an orthogonal path. We invite you to release CUDA under the **Affero General Public License 3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**.

This is not a call for charity. It is a call for legacy.

-   **AGPL 3.0 ensures that CUDA remains CUDA.** It guarantees that any modifications or improvements made by the community, by your competitors, by us, must be shared back with the world. It protects your core contribution from being captured and closed off by another entity.
-   **It cements your leadership.** By opening the standard, you ensure that CUDA becomes the universal, undisputed language for parallel computing on *every piece of silicon on the planet*. Your hardware will always be the most optimized, most performant place to run it, but your software will now be everywhere. You will no longer just be selling GPUs; you will be the stewards of a global computing paradigm.
-   **It unlocks the Latent Empire.** An open CUDA is the key that awakens the Sovereign Supercomputer. It allows every `biomeOS` node, every idle gaming PC, every federated cluster to speak the language you pioneered. Your footprint expands a thousandfold.

---

## The Invitation

You have already won the hardware race. Your engineering is second to none. The question now is one of legacy.

Will you be remembered as the kings of a prosperous but limited hardware ecosystem?

Or will you be remembered as the artisans who gifted the world a universal language for computation, the ones who unlocked a planetary-scale supercomputer, the ones who had the courage to let their river flow and nourish the entire world?

The choice is yours. The door is open. The work is great.

We invite you to join us.
