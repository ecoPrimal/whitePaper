## The Incomplete Revolution

Steam solved distribution, but the revolution isn't finished. Despite your extraordinary achievements, fundamental limitations remain:

**The Ownership Paradox:**
- Users "own" thousands of games, but can't truly control them
- Libraries are platform-locked, preventing true gaming mobility
- DRM creates artificial barriers between users and their content
- Sharing games with friends requires compromising your own access

**The Platform Prison:**
- Want to play your Steam library on your phone during lunch? Complex workarounds.
- Friend wants to try your game on their console? Impossible.
- Steam goes down? Your library is temporarily inaccessible.
- Switch platforms? Start your collection over.

**The Value Vacuum:**
- Digital items have artificial scarcity, not intrinsic value
- "Rare" skins are meaningless outside their specific game context
- No way to preserve the story of epic gaming moments
- Items can't appreciate through authentic use and achievement

These aren't criticisms—they're engineering constraints of centralized architecture. **We've built the solution.**
