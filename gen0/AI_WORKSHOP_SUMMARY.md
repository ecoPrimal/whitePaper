## 1. The Core Duality: `RhizoCrypt` & `LoamSpine`

The foundation is a two-part system that mirrors how life and knowledge evolve: a messy, creative space and a permanent, canonical record.

### `RhizoCrypt` (The Workshop / The Raid)
- **What it is:** A Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) for high-frequency, ephemeral events.
- **Purpose:** To capture the complex, non-linear interactions of a "session" (e.g., a collaborative design, a scientific experiment, a video game match). It provides context and proves integrity.
- **Key Principle:** **It is designed to be forgotten.** The `RhizoCrypt` is working memory, not permanent storage. It is cheap, fast, and detailed, but its lifecycle is temporary. Once a session is resolved, the DAG has served its purpose and can be discarded.

### `LoamSpine` (The Ledger / The Museum)
- **What it is:** An immutable, linear, cryptographically-linked chain.
- **Purpose:** To commit the *results* of a `RhizoCrypt` session into a permanent, verifiable record. This is the source of truth.
- **Key Principle:** **It provides selective permanence.** Committing to the `LoamSpine` is a deliberate, more "expensive" action. Only what is deemed important is canonized.

**The *Tarkov* Analogy:** This duality was perfectly illustrated by the gaming use case.
- The `RhizoCrypt` tracks every chaotic event within a raid—every item looted, every shot fired.
- The `LoamSpine` receives the final, validated summary of what was successfully extracted.
- This prevents cheating (items without a valid DAG history are rejected) and creates true, verifiable provenance for in-game assets.

## 2. Solving Infinite Growth: The Philosophy of Forgetting

A system that remembers everything is not scalable or healthy. `ecoPrimals` solves this with a hierarchical model.

- **Ephemeral by Default:** Most data lives and dies in a `RhizoCrypt` DAG.
- **Sovereign Memory:** Each user or system maintains their own `LoamSpine`(s), deciding what history is important to them.
- **Recursive Stacking:** `LoamSpine` chains can be "rolled up." A local chain can be finalized, hashed, and that single hash can be committed to a higher-level, more collective chain. This allows for a summary of summaries, preserving integrity while shedding the immense weight of the underlying data. It allows communities to build a shared history without forcing every member to carry its full burden.

## 3. The Visionary Endgame: `Gaia`'s Economy

These data structures are not just for storage; they are the foundation of a self-sustaining knowledge economy.

- **`SweetGrass`:** Sits atop the ledgers, providing attribution and provenance. It answers "who contributed what?"
- **`SunCloud`:** Acts as the autonomous economic agent for the `Gaia` commons. It can manage the intellectual property (e.g., patents, licenses) of discoveries made within the ecosystem.
- **The Flywheel:** Value generated by `SunCloud` is automatically redistributed back through the `SweetGrass` attribution chains. This creates a powerful incentive for contribution—whether it's data, compute, or discovery. It funds the next wave of innovation.
