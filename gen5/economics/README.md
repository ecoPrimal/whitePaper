---

## The gen5 Economic Thesis

### Zero-Cost Entry, Compounding Value

Every gen5 collaboration starts at zero cost to the collaborator's institution:

- Open-source tools (AGPL-3.0)
- Public data (PubChem, NCBI, CTF portals)
- Sovereign compute (biomeGate, ICER GPU)
- AI-accelerated pipeline coordination
- Self-verifying artifact packaging

The collaborator brings domain expertise, experimental data access, and grant PI status. The ecosystem brings validated computation.

### Value Compounds in Three Directions

1. **For the collaborator**: preliminary data for grants, validated artifacts for publications, computational infrastructure they don't maintain
2. **For the ecosystem**: new spring validation targets, new product deployment contexts, new domain coverage
3. **For the field**: open, reproducible science with provenance — every artifact is self-verifying and AGPL

### The Grant Alignment

gen5 collaborations naturally align with foundation and federal funding:

| Collaborator | Mechanism | Amount | Role Split |
|-------------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Gonzales (NF) | CTF NF Data Utilization Award | $75K/yr × 2 | Andrea = PI, Kevin = computational infrastructure |

The pattern: established PI provides domain authority and grant access. Ecosystem provides validated computation at zero cost. Preliminary data generated before a dollar is spent.
