## I. The Landscape

There is a landscape. It has a mountain, a river, and a garden.

The mountain is old. It was built over years of constrained evolution — thirteen autonomous systems, each doing one thing, each knowing only itself, each tested against its own silence until it had nothing false to say. The mountain does not move. It does not need to. Everything else flows from it.

The river begins where rain meets rock. Eight springs emerge from the mountain's geology, each carrying a different mineral — one carries heat from lattice quantum chromodynamics, one carries the slow patience of microbial ecology across eighty thousand generations, one carries the electrical signal of spectral neural networks, one carries the weight of pharmacokinetic dose-response curves. The springs converge in a delta where science proves itself against published baselines, against named tolerances, against the quiet authority of reproducible numbers.

The garden grows where the river meets the world. Products take root in the rich sediment of validated science — a verification chassis that fits on a USB drive, a sovereign game engine, a genomics pipeline for a collaborator who has never seen a primal and never will. The garden is where someone else's science comes out the other end.

And through all of it, water flows. Down from the mountain, through the river, into the garden, evaporating back into the sky to fall again. The water does not respect boundaries. It carries evolution from wherever it was created to wherever it is needed. It is the sync, the propagation, the autonomic heartbeat that keeps the ecosystem coherent without a coordinator.

This is the ecoPrimals ecosystem. These pages are its map.

## II. Three Organizations, One Landscape

The landscape has three names, because three organizations tend different altitudes:

**ecoPrimals** tends the mountain. Thirteen primals — autonomous Rust services that provide cryptography, discovery, defense, compute, compilation, storage, provenance, attribution, and orchestration. They are the infrastructure. They compose into atomic tiers: Tower (trust), Node (compute), Nest (storage), NUCLEUS (everything). When the mountain is clean — zero code debt, all tests passing, all methods registered — the river can flow.

**syntheticChemistry** tends the river. Eight springs — domain-specific validation workspaces that take primal capabilities and produce science. hotSpring validates lattice QCD against Barrick Lab MILC benchmarks. wetSpring validates microbial ecology against Lenski's 82,500-generation LTEE experiment. neuralSpring validates spectral analysis against published neural network baselines. Each spring is a tributary. Together they form the river delta — the place where computation becomes evidence.

**sporeGarden** tends the garden. Products that compose primals and springs into tools people use — lithoSpore (a 16 GB USB that reproduces LTEE science), esotericWebb (a sovereign RPG engine), helixVision (a field genomics toolkit), initioChem (computational chemistry for drug discovery). The garden is gen4: primals invisible, products visible. Gen5 is the next season: products invisible, someone else's science visible. The spore lands in new soil and grows.

## III. The Upstream-Downstream Flow

The landscape has a direction. Everything flows from the mountain to the sea.

```
                     Primal Mountain
                    (ecoPrimals — 13 primals)
                           │
                     rain meets rock
                           │
                  ┌────────┼────────┐
                  │        │        │
              hotSpring  wetSpring  neuralSpring ...
                  │        │        │
                  └────────┼────────┘
                           │
                     River Delta
                  (syntheticChemistry — 8 springs)
                           │
                  river meets the world
                           │
                  ┌────────┼────────┐
                  │        │        │
             lithoSpore  esotericWebb  helixVision ...
                  │        │        │
                  └────────┼────────┘
                           │
                     sporeGarden
                  (products — gen4/gen5)
                           │
                      the sea
                  (collaborators, the world)
```

**Upstream** means toward the mountain. When a spring discovers a gap in a primal's capabilities, that gap flows upstream as a handoff — a working document that says "rhizoCrypt needs `dag.branch`" or "nestGate needs `content.sync`." The primal team evolves. The method appears. The river carries it down.

**Downstream** means toward the garden. When a primal ships a new capability, springs absorb it into their validation suites. When springs prove a new scientific result, products crystallize it into artifacts that collaborators can hold.

**primalSpring** sits at the confluence — upstream of all springs and gardens, downstream of all primals. It does not validate domain science. It validates the coordination layer: biomeOS routing, Neural API graphs, the composition patterns that make the ecosystem more than thirteen separate programs. It publishes standards to the wateringHole, and every spring and garden downstream reads them there.

This flow is not metaphorical. It is operational. Wave coordination blurbs travel from primalSpring to mountain teams (upstream), to delta springs (midstream), to downstream products. Each wave carries evolution in both directions — new capabilities flowing down, new gaps flowing up.

## IV. Mass, Energy, Information

baseCamp Paper 23 proposes a three-way equivalence: mass (data at rest), energy (data in transit), and information (structural organization). The landscape instantiates this equivalence.

**The mountain is mass.** Primals are stored capability — frozen infrastructure, immutable when deployed. A primal binary sitting in plasmidBin is mass at rest. It does not compute. It does not validate. It waits. But it contains immense potential, like a boulder on a ridge. Thirteen boulders compose a NUCLEUS. The mountain does not move, but everything that moves comes from it.

**The river is energy.** Springs are computation in motion — validation flowing, science being done. When hotSpring runs a lattice QCD simulation, it converts mountain-mass (primal capabilities) into energy (computed observables). The river is the work happening. Every test that passes, every observable that matches a published baseline, every cross-validation check that confirms parity — that is energy flowing through the delta.

**The garden is information.** Products are structured knowledge, crystallized and portable. A pseudoSpore is not raw data and not active computation — it is the proof that computation happened correctly, sealed in BLAKE3 checksums and provenance braids. A lithoSpore USB carries not just binaries and data but the structural proof of their correctness. Information is what remains after the energy has been spent and the mass has been shaped.

And connecting them all: **water.** waterFall is the sync cycle that propagates evolution — pulling from whoever is ahead, pushing to whoever is behind, maintaining coherence without a coordinator. Spores (mass) travel on waterFall (energy). Braids (information) are the receipts of every conversion. The ferment transcript — a ~KB braid that encodes the verification power of a ~TB computation — is the ecosystem's c squared.
