## What gen5 Means

gen4 proved that primals disappear into products — esotericWebb, initioChem, blueFish, helixVision, lithoSpore. The user never sees the infrastructure. The creative surface is clean.

gen5 asks the next question: **does an external domain expert bring a real scientific question, consume the ecosystem's validated computation, and produce science they own?**

The deliverable in gen5 is not a product demo. It is not a validation check passing. It is someone else's science — packaged as a self-verifying artifact, backed by the same provenance chain, fed by real external data, and owned by the collaborator.

The spore cycle completes externally: the collaborator's science feeds back as new validation targets for springs. The ecosystem evolves from external demand, not internal reproduction.

### The Biological Metaphor

gen4 is the fruiting body — visible, differentiated, audience-facing. gen5 is the spore landing in new soil and germinating. The ecosystem reproduces itself through someone else's research.
