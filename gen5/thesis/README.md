## Purpose

gen4/thesis/ asked "now what?" after gen3 proved the science works. gen5/thesis/ asks what happens when the answer to "who uses it?" is an external domain expert whose science depends on the ecosystem.

The gen4-to-gen5 transition document lives in `gen4/thesis/THE_GEN5_TRANSITION.md` because it is the culminating thesis of gen4 — the moment gen4 products prove sufficient for external science production. This directory holds the gen5-native conceptual foundations: what changes when the ecosystem evolves from collaborator demand rather than internal reproduction.

## The gen5 Thesis in One Paragraph

gen3 proved the instruments are in tune. gen4 proved people make music with them. gen5 is when someone else's concert happens — the ecosystem provides the stage, the instruments, and the acoustics, but the music is theirs. The critical shift: springs no longer evolve only from reproducing literature. They evolve from the demands of external collaborators whose science creates new validation targets the ecosystem has never seen.
