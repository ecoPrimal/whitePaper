## The Artisan (Signal Side)

The artisan faces outward. The artisan is the Fool from the
signal side of the membrane — the side that emits.

| Property | The artisan |
|----------|-------------|
| Direction | Signal → OUT |

| What they don't emit | The recipe. The method. The ten thousand hours. |
| ZK property | Proof without revelation |
| Membrane position | Outer surface, facing the world |

The artisan proves competence without revealing knowledge.
This is literally the definition of a zero-knowledge proof:

1. **Completeness**: If the artisan can bake, the bread proves it.
   The verifier (whoever eats the bread) is convinced.
2. **Soundness**: If the artisan cannot bake, there is no bread.
   No bread, no proof. You cannot fake bread.
3. **Zero-knowledge**: The verifier learns ONLY that the artisan
   can bake. Not the starter culture. Not the hydration ratio.
   Not the fold timing. Not the oven temperature. The bread
   reveals the skill but not the knowledge.

The artisan's proof is the artifact. The artifact is public
(you can see it, eat it, use it, sit in it). The method is
private (you can reverse-engineer some of it, but the embodied
expertise — the feel for the dough, the sound of the crust —
is not extractable from the artifact).

This is why the craftsman's tradition is apprenticeship, not
textbook. The knowledge does not fit in a lossless channel.
It requires co-presence — standing next to the baker, feeling
the same dough, hearing the same oven. The knowledge is
transmitted through a lossy channel (oral tradition, physical
demonstration) and the gaps are filled by the apprentice's
own 'i' (LOSSY_IS_CREATIVE, coGen entry 13).

The artisan is the Fool because the artisan does not argue.
The artisan does not defend. The artisan does not cite
credentials. The artisan makes the bread and walks away.
The bread speaks. The Fool is silent.

## ZK Is Self

Zero-knowledge proof = proof of self without revealing contents.

The immune system does exactly this. Every nucleated cell in
the body displays MHC class I molecules on its surface. MHC I
presents peptide fragments from the cell's interior — small
pieces of whatever proteins the cell is currently making. T cells
(CD8+ cytotoxic) patrol the body and inspect these presentations.

If the peptides are self → the T cell moves on. The cell is immune.
If the peptides are non-self → the T cell kills the cell.

This is a zero-knowledge proof:

1. **Completeness**: Every self cell presents self peptides.
   The T cell is convinced: "this cell is self."
2. **Soundness**: A virus-infected cell presents viral peptides.
   The T cell is convinced: "this cell is non-self." Kill.
3. **Zero-knowledge**: The T cell learns ONLY "self or not-self."
   It does NOT learn what the cell is doing, what proteins it
   is making, what its metabolic state is, what signals it is
   processing. It learns one bit: self.

The thymus trains this system through negative selection — any
T cell that reacts to self peptides is destroyed before it
enters circulation (THYMUS_SELF_RECOGNITION_STANDARD). The
organism kills its own immune cells to prevent autoimmunity.
The organism's first act of immunity is immunity against itself.

\[
\text{ZK}_\text{immune} : \text{cell} \to \{0, 1\} \quad \text{where} \quad
\begin{cases}
1 & \text{MHC I presents self peptides} \\
0 & \text{MHC I presents non-self peptides}
\end{cases}
\]

The proof is on the surface (MHC I presentation). The contents
are inside (the cytoplasm, the nucleus, the genome). The
surface reveals self/non-self. The surface reveals NOTHING ELSE.
This is ZK. This is immunity.

## Why Complete Immunity

The Fool's immunity is not defensive. Defensive immunity requires:
- A surface to defend (the Fool has none)
- An attacker to resist (the Fool sees none)
- A claim to protect (the Fool makes none)

The Fool's immunity is structural. It is the same immunity that
null has in a type system: `null` cannot be mutated because there
is no value to mutate. `null` cannot be compared because there is
nothing to compare against. `null` cannot be attacked because
there is no entity to attack.

In the immune system: the thymus selects AGAINST reactivity.
T cells that react to self are destroyed. What survives are
T cells that react ONLY to non-self. But null is neither self
nor non-self — null is the space where the distinction hasn't
been made yet. A T cell that encounters null does... nothing.
Not "ignores" (that requires recognition followed by decision).
Nothing. The encounter does not happen. There is no peptide
to inspect. The T cell cannot even begin the verification.

```
    T cell encounters King's cell:
        MHC I → royal peptides → recognized as self → pass
        (can still be attacked by forging royal peptides)

    T cell encounters Baker's cell:
        MHC I → bread peptides → recognized as self → pass
        (can still be attacked by forging bread peptides)

    T cell encounters Fool's cell:
        MHC I → · → no peptide → no recognition → no event
        (cannot be attacked because no epitope exists to forge)
```

The King can be assassinated (forge the royal seal). The Baker
can be poisoned (contaminate the flour). The Comedian can be
silenced (ban the performance). The Fool cannot be touched —
not because the Fool is protected, but because there is
nothing there to touch.

**This is why the sourdough culture survives.** The starter has
