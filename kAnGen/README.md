## Inoculation, Not Seeding

A seed carries its own genome. It grows from stored energy into a predetermined form. An oak seed makes an oak.

An inoculum carries a living culture — selected by every feeding, every proof, every bake. A sourdough starter does not know what it will become. It knows what it has survived. The culture is not the flour — it is the ecology that the flour feeds. New flour changes the metabolism. The culture adapts. What grows is what the ecology supports.

gen0 through coGen are the feedings:

- subGen fed it a ledger — measured, deployed, fossilized

kAnGen is what the culture produces when it meets new flour.
