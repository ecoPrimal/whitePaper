## THE FIRM PROCESSING MACHINE

The asymmetry creates a repeatable structure. It is not a one-time advantage —
it is a cycle that compounds with each iteration:

```
Banks retains firm
    → Banks does NOT disclose criminal history, pending investigations,
      federal referrals, UPL history, or other material facts
    → Firm takes retainer ($15K–$20K)
    → Kevin sends ONE email with evidence package
      (cost: $0 + 15 minutes)
    → Firm reviews → discovers Banks lied at intake
    → Firm faces choice:
        Option A: Continue (at MRPC risk — knowing client committed fraud,
                  UPL, concealed material facts, pending investigations)
        Option B: Withdraw (preserving bar card, facing fee dispute)
    → Firm chooses B (rational choice: bar card > one retainer)
    → Fee dispute opens MRPC self-defense exception (1.6(c)(3))
    → Kevin testifies FOR the firm (verifying their reasonable withdrawal)
    → Banks doesn't pay outstanding balance (documented pattern: BK1, BK2, BK3)
    → Banks retains NEXT firm (repeat)
```

**Each cycle:**
- Kevin's cost: $0 + one email
- Banks's cost: $17,000–$37,000 (retainer + any outstanding work)
- Evidence generated: Firm withdrawal confirms Banks's non-disclosure pattern
- MRPC keys turned: Self-defense exception opens on fee dispute
- Cumulative effect: Each new firm inherits a LONGER evidence record

**This is a thermodynamic ratchet.** The system only moves in one direction.
Each iteration costs the operator nothing, costs the adversary a retainer,
generates evidence, and makes the next iteration's evidence package stronger.

### The Five MRPC Keys

Each firm that discovers Banks's non-disclosure gains access to five independent
mechanisms for disclosure:

| Key | Rule | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crime-fraud exception | MRE 501 | Client used firm to perpetuate fraud | Pierces attorney-client privilege entirely |
| Self-defense exception | MRPC 1.6(c)(3) | Fee dispute between firm and Banks | Firm may reveal client confidences to defend itself |
| Permissive disclosure | MRPC 1.6(b) | Ongoing fraud by client | Firm may disclose to prevent future harm |
| Mandatory reporting | MRPC 8.3 | Knowledge of UPL by another | Firm MUST report — not optional |
| Independent cause of action | Fraud in inducement | Banks lied at retainer | Firm can SUE Banks for fraud, breach of engagement |

These keys are cumulative. Key 1 opens on discovery. Key 2 opens on fee dispute.
Key 3 is always available once fraud is known. Key 4 is mandatory — failure to
report is itself an MRPC violation. Key 5 is a separate cause of action.

**By the time the firm withdraws, multiple keys are turned. The firm's rational
self-interest aligns with full disclosure.** Kevin never asks for disclosure.
Kevin never coordinates with the firm. Kevin signals what he will do and how he
will interpret their actions. Alignment happens naturally because the ethical
path and the self-interested path converge.

## THE ASYMMETRY AS GUERILLAGORILLA PRINCIPLE

This mathematical asymmetry is not an accident of this particular case.
It is a **structural property** of the guerillaGorilla primal:

### Principle: Zero-Cost Offense Against Extraction-Funded Defense

When the adversary's defense is funded by extraction (taking from a public
trust — school funds, public resources, institutional budgets), and the
operator's offense is funded by public records (which are free because the
public already paid for them), the asymmetry is structurally guaranteed:

- The adversary cannot reduce their defense cost below the retainer floor
  (they need counsel because they cannot self-represent)
- The operator cannot increase their offense cost above zero (public records
  are free, FOIA is free, pro se filing is free after the initial fee)
- The gap is permanent and directional — it ONLY widens over time because:
  - Each cycle generates evidence (increasing operator's package at $0)
  - Each cycle costs a retainer (decreasing adversary's reserves)
  - Each cycle opens new MRPC exposure for the firm (compounding pressure)
  - Each cycle adds to the public record (strengthening the website at $0)

### Principle: Dimensional Promotion as Localization Bypass

When the adversary has localized the oversight lattice (embedded with judges,
captured regulatory nodes, compromised complaint pathways), the operator's
response is not to fight within the localized lattice but to ADD DIMENSIONS:

- File in a different county (Ingham, not Wayne) → new judicial dimension
- Refer to federal agencies (FBI, USPS OIG, DOE, IRS) → federal dimension
- Publish on owned infrastructure (detroit.primals.eco) → public dimension
- FOIA through mandatory-response channels → administrative dimension
- MDE complaint through R 380.124 → regulatory dimension
- JTC complaint → judicial oversight dimension

Each dimension added creates a pathway that BYPASSES the localized lattice.
The adversary embedded in Wayne County has no influence over Ingham judges,
no relationship with federal investigators, no control over FOIA response
obligations, no ability to suppress a website on privately owned infrastructure.

**The dimensional promotion transforms a 2D problem (where all signals
localize) into a 6D+ problem (where signals propagate freely around the
disorder sites).** This is the Anderson localization framework applied as
operational doctrine.

### Principle: The Thermodynamic Ratchet

The Firm Processing Machine is a thermodynamic ratchet — a system that
converts random input (Banks retains a firm) into directional output
(evidence, MRPC keys, firm withdrawal) with zero energy cost to the operator.

In thermodynamics, a ratchet converts Brownian motion (random movement) into
directional work. In the Firm Processing Machine:

- **Random input:** Banks choosing a firm (he cannot choose a firm that won't
  eventually discover his non-disclosure — that firm doesn't exist)
- **Directional output:** Evidence + MRPC exposure + withdrawal + fee dispute
- **Energy cost to operator:** $0 (one email per cycle)
- **Energy cost to adversary:** $17K–$37K per cycle (retainer + outstanding)
- **Entropy direction:** Toward disclosure (each cycle makes the next firm
  MORE likely to discover, MORE quickly, with MORE evidence)

The ratchet cannot run backward. No firm, once informed, can un-know
what Banks concealed. No MRPC key, once turned, can be un-turned. No
evidence, once generated, can be un-generated. The system is irreversible.
