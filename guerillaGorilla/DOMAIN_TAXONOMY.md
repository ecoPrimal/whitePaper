# guerillaGorilla — Domain Taxonomy

**Every subsystem of legal existence the primal covers.**
Each subsystem has defined inputs, outputs, and validation criteria.

---

## THE SEVEN SUBSYSTEMS

```
guerillaGorilla
  │
  ├── 1. IDENTITY          — documents that prove you exist
  ├── 2. RIGHTS            — legal frameworks that protect you
  ├── 3. FINANCE           — systems that determine economic existence
```

## 1. IDENTITY — Documents That Prove You Exist

The foundation layer. Without identity documents, no other subsystem functions.

### Scope

| Document | What it enables | What happens without it |
|---|---|---|
| Birth certificate | Proves citizenship, age, parentage | Can't get passport, can't prove parentage in custody |
| Social Security card/number | Employment, taxes, benefits, credit | Can't work legally, can't file taxes |
| Driver's license / state ID | Identification, driving, age verification | Can't rent, can't fly, can't verify identity |
| DD-214 (military discharge) | Veteran benefits, employment preference | Lost VA benefits, lost hiring preference |
| Passport | International travel, secondary ID | Can't leave the country |
| Marriage license / divorce decree | Marital status, property rights | Ambiguous asset division, no spousal rights |
| Custody order | Parental rights, school access, medical consent | No standing as a parent in any institution |

### Inputs
- Knowledge of what documents exist and where they're held
- Adversary identification (who controls the documents)
- Replacement procedures (vital records offices, SSA, DMV, NPRC)

### Outputs
- Recovered or replaced documents in the operator/client's possession
- Documented chain of custody (who held them, how recovered)
- Protective measures (certified copies, secure storage)

### Validation
- Document in hand: yes/no
- Institution accepts it: yes/no (school recognizes custody order, employer accepts SSN)

### Case validation (DaSean Mitchell)
- DD-214, SSN card, birth certificate locked in Shayla's safe → recovery pending
- No custody order for 9 years → emergency custody motion filed Sep 3
- School (PCA) refused father access → parental authorization filed Aug 29

## 2. RIGHTS — Legal Frameworks That Protect You

The rights layer defines what the law says you can do, access, and demand.

### Scope

| Right | Source | What it enables |
|---|---|---|
| Parental rights | MCL 722.21 et seq. (Child Custody Act) | Access to children, school records, medical decisions |
| School record access | FERPA (20 USC § 1232g), MCL 380.1137 | Parent's right to inspect education records |
| Custody standing | MCL 722.27 | Right to petition for custody modification |
| Authorized representative | Parental authorization letter | Third party can act on parent's behalf at institutions |
| Benefits information | MDHHS regulations | Right to know what benefits are claimed in your name |
| Mail privacy | 18 USC § 1708 | Criminal protection against mail theft/interception |
| Communication | Michigan eavesdropping statute MCL 750.539c | Protection against unauthorized monitoring |

### Inputs
- Identification of applicable statutes and regulations
- Factual situation (what rights are being violated)
- Institutional interface requirements (what form, what process)

### Outputs
- Rights assertion letters/filings
- Institutional compliance (school provides records, agency provides information)
- Documentation of rights violations (evidence for subsystem 6)

### Validation
- Institution complied: yes/no
- Right exercised: yes/no
- Violation documented for escalation: yes/no

### Case validation
- FERPA right to school records → Banks denied access → violation documented
- Parental authorization → filed with PCA → Banks rejected ("I'm a lawyer") → UPL claim
- Mail privacy → Marquita intercepted certified mail → OIG referral filed

## 3. FINANCE — Systems That Determine Economic Existence

The financial layer covers everything that determines whether you can earn,
save, borrow, and be protected from exploitation.

### Scope

| System | Function | Exploitation vector |
|---|---|---|
| Bank accounts | Hold and access money | Joint accounts drained, unauthorized signers |
| Credit reports | Determine borrowing ability | Unauthorized debts, identity-based credit abuse |
| Benefits (SNAP, Medicaid) | Safety net | Benefits claimed in your name without knowledge |
| Tax filings | Income reporting, refunds | Filed without consent, refund intercepted |
| Employment records | Income history | Undocumented income diversion |
| Child support | Financial obligation | Support ordered based on manipulated information |
| Debt | Obligations | Debts incurred in your name without consent |

### Inputs
- Financial records (bank statements, tax returns, credit reports)
- Benefits records (MDHHS, SSA)
- Employment records
- Adversary financial records (bankruptcy filings, property records)

### Outputs
- Financial separation from exploitative household
- Credit repair filings
- Benefits fraud documentation
- Discovery instruments for financial records
- Bankruptcy analysis (creditor lists, asset schedules, undisclosed assets)

### Validation
- Accounts separated: yes/no
- Benefits corrected: yes/no
- Financial exploitation quantified: dollar amount documented

### Case validation
- DaSean's phone bill: $40 → $1,000/mo (Shayla adding family) → quantified
- Marquita's 3 bankruptcies: undisclosed Jeep, undisclosed address → trustee notified
- Smith household financial flows: PACER, ROD, credit records → mapped

## 4. PROTECTION — Apparatus That Shields From Harm

The protective layer provides immediate safety through legal instruments.

### Scope

| Instrument | Court | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Protection Order (PPO) | Circuit Court | No-contact, stay-away, anti-stalking |
| Emergency custody order | Family Court (ex parte) | Immediate temporary custody |
| Criminal complaint | Prosecutor / police | Assault, theft, fraud charges |
| Federal criminal referral | OIG, FBI, USPS Inspection | Mail fraud, wire fraud, benefits fraud |
| Restraining order | Circuit Court | Asset freeze, document preservation |

### Inputs
- Evidence of harm (video, witness statements, medical records)
- Correct forms for jurisdiction (CC 375, 376, 380, 381 for Michigan PPOs)
- Respondent identification (name, DOB, address, relationship)

### Outputs
- Filed and served protective orders
- Emergency custody orders
- Criminal complaints with case/report numbers
- Safety plans

### Validation
- Order issued: yes/no
- Order served: yes/no
- Violation documented: yes/no (for contempt proceedings)

### Case validation
- Ring video Sep 2: Leon + Shayla assault documented → 4 PPOs filed (Ingham County)
- Emergency custody motion: filed via 3rdcc Sep 3
- OIG referral: Leon Smith USPS mail interception → filed

### Cross-protection extension (Mode 2)

Protection extends beyond individual instruments when operators share fEAR.
See `CROSS_PROTECTION.md`. Two operators with overlapping adversary awareness
create **Mutual Assured Documentation** — structural deterrence where silencing
one operator triggers the other's documentation apparatus. The adversary cannot
remove the recorder without activating a second recorder.

- Proactive defense: filings create protected activity timestamps; all subsequent
  adversary actions measured against them (retaliation becomes evidence)
- Retaliatory filing detection: fEAR senses temporal proximity between operator's
  filings and adversary's responsive actions
- Cross-documentation: if one operator is silenced (incarceration, injunction,
  manufactured contempt), the other's documentation continues

## 5. NAVIGATION — Process of Moving Through Institutions

The navigation layer is the operational knowledge of how to actually
interact with courts, agencies, and institutions — the mise en place
of legal existence.

### Scope

| System | What you need to know |
|---|---|
| Court e-filing (3rdcc, mi-file) | Platform, login, document format, payment (Allpaid) |
| Process service | Who can serve, how to file proof, alternative service |
| FOIA requests | Correct agency, correct statute (MCL 15.231), fee structure |
| Notarization | When required (JTC), where to get it, what it costs |
| Certified mail | When required, tracking, proof of delivery/refusal |
| Agency complaints | Correct form, correct office, correct jurisdiction |
| Court scheduling | How to request hearing dates, motion practice, CMC procedures |
| Filing fees | Fee waivers (MCR 2.002), fee schedule, payment methods |

### Inputs
- Identification of correct institution and process
- Required forms and formats
- Deadlines and scheduling requirements

### Outputs
- Correctly filed documents with receipts/case numbers
- Process service affidavits
- FOIA requests with tracking
- Calendared deadlines

### Validation
- Filing accepted: yes/no (rejected filings are bugs — fix and refile)
- Deadline met: yes/no
- Service completed: yes/no

### Case validation
- 3rdcc e-filing walkthrough: emergency motion filed successfully
- Process service: Banks served at PCA and home via ABC Process Service

## 6. ACCOUNTABILITY — Adversarial Response System

The accountability layer is the fEAR-driven adversarial system that
holds others to account. This is where litigation lives, but it's broader
than lawsuits.

### Scope

| Channel | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Civil litigation | Damages, injunctions, declaratory relief | Mok v. Banks (2026-4349-CZ), Mok v. Smith (2026-4301-CZ) |
| Regulatory complaints | Agency investigation and enforcement | JTC (Miller, Yancey), State Bar (UPL), MDE (cert violation) |
| Criminal referrals | Prosecution by state/federal authorities | AG referral (Banks UPL), OIG (Leon mail), FBI/DOJ (RICO) |
| OSINT | Intelligence gathering from public sources | Network mapping, financial analysis, connection identification |
| Press | Public accountability through journalism | Rita Williams (Discord), Elrick (historical) |
| Discovery | Compelled evidence production | Interrogatories, document requests, depositions, subpoenas |

### Inputs
- Evidence from all other subsystems
- Adversary behavior (fEAR sensing loop)
- Legal framework (applicable statutes, court rules)

### Outputs
- Filed complaints and suits
- Regulatory complaint case numbers
- OSINT briefs and network maps
- Press packages for journalists
- Discovery instruments

### Validation
- Filing accepted: yes/no
- Agency acted: yes/no (investigation opened, notice sent)
- Evidence admissible: yes/no
- Network map accurate: verified against primary sources

### Case validation
- 5 regulatory complaints filed → awaiting institutional responses
- Banks civil suit filed and served → 21-day answer deadline running
- OSINT brief: 60+ people mapped, all sourced, reproducible

## 7. FORMALIZATION — Converting Informal to Formal

The formalization layer is the core thesis of fEAR applied to legal
existence. It is documented in detail in `../subGen/FEAR_INFORMAL_TO_FORMAL.md`.

### The Principle

Exploitation thrives on informality. The employer who pays cash. The partner
who controls finances without a joint account. The family that makes custody
decisions without a court order. Informality serves the party with more power.

guerillaGorilla converts informal arrangements into formal claims:
- Informal financial exploitation → quantified damages claim
- Informal custody arrangement → formal custody petition
- Informal identity document control → formal recovery action
- Informal consent violations → documented consent timeline
- Informal institutional capture → regulatory complaints with citations

### Inputs
- Informal arrangements (undocumented, unwritten, assumed)
- Adversary's own documentation of those arrangements (exhibits, filings, social media)
- Primary source records that formalize what was informal (PACER, ROD, LARA)

### Outputs
- Formal claims with quantified damages
- Consent timeline (was consent obtained? prove it.)
- Discovery instruments compelling formalization (produce the agreement, the authorization, the record)

### Validation
- Was the informal arrangement accurately characterized? (verified against multiple sources)
- Is the formal claim legally cognizable? (statute + case law support)
- Has the adversary's own documentation been used against them? (self-incrimination via sworn exhibits)

### Case validation
- Shayla's 192-page filing: formalized her own exploitation (EBT invoices, eavesdropping admission)
- 9 years of undocumented financial exploitation → quantified damages with receipts
- No PS Form 3801 for mail → federal mail fraud (formalization converts absence into evidence)

## SUBSYSTEM INTERACTIONS

The seven subsystems are not independent. They feed each other continuously:

```
IDENTITY ──────→ RIGHTS (can't exercise rights without proving identity)
   │                │
   │                ▼
   │            FINANCE (can't access financial systems without identity + rights)
   │                │
   │                ▼
   │          PROTECTION (can't get protective orders without documented harm)
   │                │
   │                ▼
   │          NAVIGATION (can't file without knowing the process)
   │                │
   │                ▼
   │        ACCOUNTABILITY (can't hold accountable without evidence from all above)
   │                │
   │                ▼
   └──────── FORMALIZATION (converts all of the above from informal to formal)
                    │
                    ▼
              (feeds back into every subsystem with new formal claims)
```

DaSean's case traversed all seven in one week:
1. **Identity**: Parental authorization filed (Aug 29)
2. **Rights**: FERPA access demanded at PCA (Aug 29)
3. **Finance**: Smith household exploitation quantified (ongoing)
4. **Protection**: PPOs filed, emergency custody (Sep 2-3)
5. **Navigation**: 3rdcc e-filing, Ingham County filing, Wayne County filing
6. **Accountability**: Banks suit, Smith suit, 5 regulatory complaints
7. **Formalization**: 9 years of informal exploitation → 8+ formal proceedings
