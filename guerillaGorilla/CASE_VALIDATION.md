## THE EXPERIMENT

Like every baseCamp paper, guerillaGorilla must validate against reality.

- baseCamp Paper 01 validates wetSpring against published DADA2 results
- baseCamp Paper 14 validates hotSpring against Necco-Sommer lattice QCD
- **baseCamp Paper 28 validates guerillaGorilla against a live adversarial system**

The methodology is identical:
1. Published claim (adversary's representation of reality)
2. Independent reproduction (guerillaGorilla's investigation)
3. Comparison (discrepancy analysis)
4. If discrepancy: determine who is wrong (primary sources decide)

| Domain | Published claim | Reproduction | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lattice QCD | ⟨P⟩ at β=6.0 (Necco-Sommer) | hotSpring SU(3) measurement | 0.3% agreement → reality confirmed |
| Legal | "Dr. Brian Banks, J.D., Ph.D." | State Bar search → zero results | Discrepancy → Banks is wrong |
| Legal | Banks runs school as "superintendent" | Cert has cash-handling prohibition | Discrepancy → violation |
| Legal | Shayla claims "DaSean is aware" | No documentation of notification | Discrepancy → consent violated |
| Legal | Marquita's BK lists Bradford address | Children enrolled from Dresden | Discrepancy → address fraud |

### Days 23–25 (Sep 22): Consolidation + Intelligence Build

**STRIDe: Strategy reassessment (major architectural evolution)**

The case has reached a complexity threshold that demands new infrastructure. The operator
has filed in 2 counties, 8+ agencies, and 5 PPO cases — with 10+ active proceedings and
124+ case artifacts. Navigation by memory is no longer possible.

**Three emergent capabilities of the primal activated:**

**1. fossilRecord — Epoch-Based Provenance Archive**

The primal's artifact output exceeds working memory. Solution: archival system that
preserves provenance while reducing active surface area.

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Active documents | 168 | 44 |
| Archived to fossilRecord | — | 124 |
| Epochs defined | — | 3 (Foundation, Agency Blitz, Service Battle) |
| Archive READMEs | — | 3 (with supersession chains documented) |

This maps directly to the **rhizoCrypt** provenance layer: immutable past state,
organized by temporal epoch, with every document's supersession relationship traced.
Git history preserves the full chain. The fossilRecord is the legal rhizoCrypt.

**2. CASE_REGISTRY — Relational Source of Truth**

10+ proceedings means 10+ sets of case numbers, parties, judges, deadlines, and
roles. Duplication across documents creates inconsistency risk. Solution: central
relational index.

The CASE_REGISTRY contains:
- All case IDs with standardized formats (civil: `2026-XXXX-CZ`, PPO: `26-0000004XXX-PP/PH`, custody: `26-XXXXXX-DC`)
- All parties with typed roles per case (plaintiff, petitioner, respondent, authorized rep, witness, expert)
- All judges with case assignments and hearing schedules
- Kevin ↔ DaSean relationship formally specified: "best friends, fellow veterans. NOT biological brothers."
- Kevin's roles per case explicitly typed: where he is party (pro se), where he is authorized rep + witness + expert
- Expert qualifications table (MRE 702 / FRE 702): military intelligence, data science, microbiology, Sandia Fellow

This maps to the **loamSpine** certificate layer: typed metadata, case-level relationships,
role-scoped access (who can file what, where). The CASE_REGISTRY is the legal loamSpine.

**3. GRAPH_DATA / GRAPH_SCHEMA — Entity-Relationship Intelligence**

The enterprise prosecution theory requires network visualization: who is connected to
whom, through what entities, with what temporal evidence. Solution: reusable graph schema
with concrete data instantiation.

Schema defines:
- 10 node types (person, entity, case, event, property, motion, agency, evidence, judge, school)
- 17 edge types (filed_in, party_to, employed_by, controls, related_to, triggered_by,
  evidence_of, served_on, evasion_of, authorized_rep_for, assigned_to, etc.)
- Temporal properties on all edges (start_date, end_date, discovered_date)
- Role typing on party_to edges (plaintiff, defendant, petitioner, respondent, witness,
  expert, auth_rep) with pro_se and appears_in_person booleans

Data instantiates 25+ person nodes, 10+ entity nodes, 10+ case nodes, 30+ events,
and 100+ typed edges — all sourced from primary records (PACER, ROD, LARA, ICHAT,
court filings).

This maps to the **sweetGrass** attribution layer: network of actors and actions,
with temporal discovery tracking. The graph intelligence is the legal sweetGrass.

**fEAR: Evolution (the provenance trio emerges in law)**

The three emergent capabilities — fossilRecord, CASE_REGISTRY, GRAPH_DATA — are
the **provenance trio instantiated in the legal domain:**

| Provenance layer | ecoPrimals | guerillaGorilla (legal) |
|---|---|---|
| **rhizoCrypt** (past) | Immutable DAG of system history | fossilRecord: epoch-based archive with git-preserved history |
| **loamSpine** (certificates) | Typed metadata, role-scoped access | CASE_REGISTRY: typed case metadata, role-scoped filing authority |
| **sweetGrass** (attribution) | Network of who did what, when | GRAPH_DATA: entity-relationship intelligence with temporal edges |

**This was not designed.** It emerged from the operational demands of managing a
multi-party, multi-jurisdiction enterprise prosecution. The constraint (10+ cases,
2 counties, 124+ artifacts, 5 respondents) reshaped the fitness landscape so that
the same provenance architecture validated in gaming (Paper 20), science (Paper 21),
and medical (Paper 22) domains emerged independently in law.

**This is the constrained evolution thesis in action: the same constraints produce
the same architectures, regardless of domain.**

## EMERGENT CAPABILITIES — UNNAMED (Week 4+)

The following capabilities emerged from operational pressure. They were not designed
into the primal's architecture. They are being documented here for future naming
and formalization.

### 1. Epoch Archival (working name: fossilRecord)

When the artifact count exceeds working memory (~50-60 documents), the primal
spontaneously produces an archival system: documents are classified by temporal epoch,
moved to immutable storage with supersession chains documented, and replaced by
consolidated successor documents. The active surface area contracts while total
information is preserved.

**Biological analog:** Lenski's frozen fossil record. Each epoch is a population
sample frozen at a defined timepoint. The living culture continues to evolve; the
frozen samples preserve the historical state for future analysis.

**Provenance mapping:** rhizoCrypt (immutable DAG of past state).

### 2. Relational Index (working name: CASE_REGISTRY)

When the case count exceeds mental coordination capacity (~5-7 proceedings), the
primal spontaneously produces a relational source of truth: case numbers, parties,
judges, roles, and deadlines centralized with typed cross-references. Duplication
eliminated. All other documents reference the registry by ID rather than repeating
metadata.

**Computational analog:** Database normalization. When data is duplicated across
tables (documents), inconsistencies accumulate. Normalization extracts shared data
into a single table (registry) referenced by key.

**Provenance mapping:** loamSpine (typed certificates, role-scoped access).

### 3. Graph Intelligence (working name: GRAPH_DATA / GRAPH_SCHEMA)

When the adversary network exceeds pattern recognition from flat lists (~15-20
connected actors), the primal spontaneously produces a graph: typed nodes for
entities, typed edges for relationships, temporal properties for intelligence
accumulation, and a reusable schema separating structure from data.

**Computational analog:** The graph schema is domain-agnostic. The same schema
(node types, edge types, temporal properties) applies to any adversary network
in any jurisdiction. The data layer instantiates it for a specific case. New
cases inherit the schema and produce new data.

**Provenance mapping:** sweetGrass (attribution network, who did what when).

### 4. Multi-Channel Service as Selection Experiment

When traditional service fails, the primal does not "try harder on the same
channel." It treats service as a **selection experiment**: multiple independent
channels tested simultaneously. Each channel produces one of two outcomes:
(a) service achieved, or (b) evasion documented. Both outcomes are fitness-positive
for the operator. The adversary cannot resist without generating evidence.

**Biological analog:** Antibiotic resistance testing. Multiple antibiotics applied
simultaneously. Each reveals a sensitivity or resistance — both are diagnostic.

**Legal formalization:** Alternative service motions. The 7-channel blast documents
good-faith effort AND adversary evasion in a single operation.

### 5. Proactive Defense Timeline as Armor

The primal's filing sequence creates an expanding timeline of "protected activity"
timestamps. Every subsequent adversary action is automatically measured against this
timeline. Retaliation is not detected — it is **classified** by temporal proximity to
the operator's filings. The armor was in place before the retaliation started.

**Week 4 state:** 20+ protected activity timestamps spanning 25 days. Any adversary
action is now within retaliatory proximity to at least 3 filings.

### 6. Authorized Representative as Legal Primal Type

The primal evolved a new role type: **authorized representative** — a non-attorney
who files on behalf of a party, appears as fact witness and expert witness, but
explicitly is NOT the party's attorney. This is a distinct legal entity type with
specific scope constraints:
- Files documents on behalf of petitioner
- Testifies as fact witness (observed events) and expert witness (domain qualifications)
- Does NOT provide legal advice, legal representation, or attorney-client privilege
- Party appears in person at hearings; representative handles filing logistics

This role type is formalized in CASE_REGISTRY and GRAPH_SCHEMA (`authorized_rep_for`
edge type with `scope`, `cases[]`, and `NOT_ATTORNEY=true` properties).
