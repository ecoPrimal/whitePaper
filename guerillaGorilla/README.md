## What Is guerillaGorilla?

guerillaGorilla is the legal primal in the ecoPrimals ecosystem. It occupies
the same architectural position as barraCuda (GPU compute), wetSpring
(bioinformatics), or hotSpring (physics) — but its domain is **legal existence
as infrastructure.**

It is not a litigation tool. It is not "how to sue someone." It is the
complete apparatus by which a person exists formally in a system designed
to be navigated by attorneys, when they don't have one.

| What other primals do | What guerillaGorilla does |
|---|---|
| barraCuda dispatches to GPU for physics | guerillaGorilla dispatches to squirrel for legal intelligence |
| wetSpring pipelines sequencing reads through validation | guerillaGorilla pipelines public records through network analysis |
| hotSpring reproduces lattice QCD against published results | guerillaGorilla reproduces claims against primary sources |
| All primals produce self-verifying artifacts | guerillaGorilla produces provenance-traced legal filings |

The gorilla is the legal apparatus. The squirrel is the brain it borrows.
The operator is the one who decides where to walk.

## The Name

**guerilla** — Asymmetric warfare. Small force against large institution.
No standing army, no law firm, no institutional backing. The operator
uses terrain (public records), tempo (AI-accelerated analysis), and
information asymmetry (OSINT the adversary doesn't know exists) to
outmaneuver opponents with vastly greater nominal resources.

**Gorilla** — Silverback. Overwhelming physical presence that belies
patient intelligence. The gorilla does not chase. It sits. It watches.
It understands the social structure of the group. When it moves, the
entire dynamic changes. One filing reorganizes every relationship in
the network. One brief reveals that 60+ people have been mapped. The
gorilla is not fast. It is heavy.

**guerillaGorilla** — The compound: asymmetric tactics deployed with
overwhelming analytical weight. Fast like a guerilla. Heavy like a
gorilla. The paradox is the point — the system is simultaneously
nimble (10 hours per filing) and crushing (1,100-line OSINT brief,
60+ person network map, 5 simultaneous regulatory complaints).

The lowercase-uppercase split follows ecoPrimals naming convention
(ecoPrimals, barraCuda, wetSpring, hotSpring). The first word is the
modifier. The second is the species.
