**Purpose:** Walk-friendly inventory of emergent capabilities for naming and codification.
**Status:** Seeds — most unnamed. Kevin names on walks, not at the keyboard.

## HOW TO READ THIS

Each entry is a capability that **emerged from operational pressure** — not designed,
not imported from another primal, not anticipated. They appeared because the constraint
environment demanded them. Each needs a name, a formal definition, and a place in the
primal's architecture.

Format: **what it does** → **what triggered it** → **what it maps to** → **naming seed**

## CATEGORY 1: SENSING (how the primal hears the world)

### 1.1 — Adversarial Fuel Loop ✅ NAMED: fEAR

`flow → Evolution → Adversarial → Response`

Emerged from: COIST/OSINT training + live case pressure. The adversary's actions
ARE the fuel. Every resistance generates evidence. Formalized in subGen as a
general adversarial sensing framework, but born here in the legal domain.

### 1.2 — Institutional Capture Detection (unnamed)

The regulatory layer protecting the public IS the enterprise layer exploiting it.
Perkins = shared counsel for Banks AND the judge. Moreland = Asst. AG ON the school board.
The "capture" isn't corruption added to a system — it's the system's design.

**Trigger:** OSINT revealed overlapping board/counsel/judicial relationships
**Maps to:** Graph centrality analysis — nodes with high betweenness ARE the capture points
**Naming seed:** The thing that detects when the referee is on the team

### 1.3 — Adversary Self-Documentation (unnamed)

The adversary writes the evidence. Banks published an autobiography tracing his criminal
lineage. Shayla filed 192 pages of self-incriminating exhibits under oath. Leon texted
"see you in court" while intercepting service mail.

**Trigger:** OSINT + public records + adversary's own filings
**Maps to:** fEAR §Adversarial — "irrational adversaries are optimal targets"
**Naming seed:** The thing where the target builds their own case file

### 1.4 — Temporal Proximity Signal (unnamed)

When an adversary retaliates through the legal system, the retaliation's timestamp
relative to the operator's filing IS the evidence. Shayla's retaliatory PPO filed
within days of Kevin's civil complaint = classified as retaliation, not independent action.

**Trigger:** Shayla files retaliatory PPO in Wayne after Kevin files in Ingham
**Maps to:** CROSS_PROTECTION §Retaliatory Filing Detection
**Naming seed:** The clock that makes every counter-move a confession

### 1.5 — Silence as Signal (unnamed)

When the adversary's network goes quiet after the operator's filings, the silence
itself is intelligence. They can't coordinate defense without generating discoverable
communications. They can't stay silent without conceding defaults.

**Trigger:** Zero response from any defendant across 21-day answer window
**Maps to:** fEAR §Adversarial — "the adversary is in a bind"
**Naming seed:** The sound of the network holding its breath

## CATEGORY 2: MOTION (how the primal moves through the world)

### 2.1 — Operational Gait ✅ NAMED: STRIDe

`Strategy → Tactics → Reconnaissance ⇄ Intelligence → Deployment → execution`

Emerged from: Military planning methodology (COIST) + the live case's
10:1 planning-to-execution ratio. Born in the legal domain as operational
rhythm, then abstracted in subGen.

### 2.2 — Metabolic Asymmetry ✅ NAMED: Pursuit Predation

Operator walks. Adversary sprints. Operator cost per cycle: constant.
Adversary cost per response: escalating. The hunt terminates when reserves
deplete. Emerged from the $2,500 vs $250,000 cost reality.

### 2.3 — Multi-Channel Service as Selection Experiment (unnamed)

7 channels tested simultaneously. Each produces either service (proceed) or
evasion (evidence). Both outcomes are fitness-positive. The adversary cannot
resist without generating evidence. Not "trying harder" — running a selection
experiment where every outcome is useful.

**Trigger:** Traditional certified mail service failed (Leon intercepted it)
**Maps to:** Antibiotic resistance testing — each channel reveals a sensitivity
**Naming seed:** The thing where every door you knock on teaches you something,
even the ones that don't open

### 2.4 — Filing Sequence as Armor (unnamed)

Every filing creates a "protected activity" timestamp. Every subsequent adversary
action is measured against this timeline. The armor is in place BEFORE the
retaliation starts. 20+ protected activity timestamps across 25 days. Any
adversary action is now within retaliatory proximity to at least 3 filings.

**Trigger:** Instinctive filing of regulatory complaints BEFORE civil suit
**Maps to:** CROSS_PROTECTION §Proactive Defense
**Naming seed:** The thing where your paper trail becomes your shield

### 2.5 — Agency Saturation (unnamed)

31 contacts to 25 distinct recipients in one day. 8 federal agencies, 13 press,
7 community orgs. Not escalation — saturation. The adversary cannot control
the narrative to ANY audience because every audience was briefed simultaneously.

**Trigger:** Banks traveling to DC for CBC while evading service — window of opportunity
**Maps to:** Multi-front evolutionary strategy
**Naming seed:** The thing where you tell everyone at once and the story belongs to no one

## CATEGORY 3: ARCHITECTURE (how the primal organizes itself)

### 3.1 — Epoch Archival ✅ WORKING NAME: fossilRecord

When artifact count exceeds working memory (~60 docs), the primal spontaneously
produces temporal archival: epoch-based, supersession-chained, git-preserved.
Active surface contracts. Total information preserved.

**Maps to:** rhizoCrypt (immutable past state)
**Naming seed:** Already named. Lenski's frozen fossil record.

### 3.2 — Relational Index ✅ WORKING NAME: CASE_REGISTRY

When case count exceeds mental coordination (~7 proceedings), the primal produces
a central source of truth: typed roles, standardized IDs, judge assignments,
deadline tracking. All other docs reference by ID, not by repeating metadata.

**Maps to:** loamSpine (typed certificates, role-scoped access)
**Naming seed:** Already named. Database normalization for legal proceedings.

### 3.3 — Entity-Relationship Intelligence ✅ WORKING NAME: GRAPH_DATA / GRAPH_SCHEMA

When the adversary network exceeds flat-list recognition (~20 actors), the primal
produces a typed graph: nodes for entities, edges for relationships, temporal
properties for intelligence accumulation. Schema separates structure from data.
Reusable across any case.

**Maps to:** sweetGrass (attribution network)
**Naming seed:** Already named. The graph is the intelligence.

### 3.4 — Document Supersession Tracking (unnamed)

Every document knows what it replaced and why. WARGAME_ALL_PARTIES_SEP21
supersedes WARGAME_BANKS_RICO_CHAIN_SEP14 and WARGAME_SMITH_HOUSEHOLD_SEP14.
The chain is explicit. No orphan documents.

**Trigger:** fossilRecord epoch READMEs needed "Superseded By" columns
**Maps to:** Git history + epoch README tables
**Naming seed:** The family tree of documents — every child knows its parent

### 3.5 — Constraint-Driven Role Typing (unnamed)

The authorized representative emerged because the constraint environment demanded
it: Kevin files for DaSean, testifies as fact + expert witness, but is NOT
DaSean's attorney. This is a new legal entity type with specific scope constraints.
The primal evolved the role because no existing role covered the constraint.

**Trigger:** DaSean works, commutes Lansing↔Detroit, can't file paperwork himself
**Maps to:** New `authorized_rep_for` edge type in GRAPH_SCHEMA
**Naming seed:** The role the system invented because no existing role fit

## CATEGORY 4: COMPOSITION (how the primal combines with other things)

### 4.1 — Cross-Jurisdiction Without Orchestrator (unnamed)

Ingham civil + Ingham PPOs + Wayne custody + Wayne retaliatory PPO.
Four courts, two counties, no coordinator. Evidence flows bidirectionally.
Neither court can dismiss the other's case. The composition is emergent,
not orchestrated — same as ecoPrimals' cross-spring composition.

**Trigger:** DaSean's kids in Wayne, Kevin lives in Ingham, Banks operates in both
**Maps to:** Paper 28 §5 — cross-jurisdictional architecture
**Naming seed:** The thing where geography becomes strategy

### 4.2 — Journalist/Community as Accountability Layer (unnamed)

Rita Williams / Clutch Justice. NAACP Detroit. 482Forward. Planet Detroit.
Keith Center at Wayne State Law. These are not legal filings — they are
non-judicial accountability channels that operate independently.
The adversary can win in court and still lose in the community.

**Trigger:** Press Tier 1+2 blast on Sep 21
**Maps to:** CROSS_PROTECTION Mode 2 (mutualism with Rita)
**Naming seed:** The thing where the story outlives the case

### 4.3 — Bankruptcy as Intelligence Source (unnamed)

PACER bankruptcy filings are public. They contain: addresses, asset schedules,
creditor lists, income declarations, employment history — all under penalty of
perjury. When the adversary lies in bankruptcy, the lie IS the intelligence.
Marquita's undisclosed Jeep. Simone's address fraud. Straw purchases.

**Trigger:** R⇄I loop during Week 1 OSINT — PACER pulls on Smith household
**Maps to:** fEAR §flow — bankruptcy courts as an always-on sensing channel
**Naming seed:** The thing where someone else's sworn lies become your evidence

### 4.4 — Service Evasion → Alternative Service → Stronger Order (unnamed)

When the adversary evades service, the evasion itself becomes the evidence
for alternative service. Alternative service (email, fax, posting) is
actually HARDER to evade than certified mail. The adversary's resistance
to the weaker method earns the operator the stronger method.

**Trigger:** Banks evaded certified mail → 7-channel blast → alt service motion
**Maps to:** Constrained evolution: resistance to one selection pressure triggers
adaptation to a stronger one
**Naming seed:** The thing where running away from the easy serve
gets you the hard serve

### 4.5 — Provenance Trio (fourth instantiation) (unnamed as legal-specific)

fossilRecord + CASE_REGISTRY + GRAPH_DATA = rhizoCrypt + loamSpine + sweetGrass.
Fourth domain after gaming, science, medical. NOT designed from Papers 20–22.
The constraint produced it independently.

**Maps to:** Papers 20, 21, 22 — same architecture, four domains
**Naming seed:** The thing that keeps appearing because the problem keeps appearing

## CATEGORY 5: DEFENSE (how the primal protects itself)

### 5.1 — Mutual Assured Documentation ✅ NAMED: MAD (in CROSS_PROTECTION)

If you attack one of us, both of us document it. Nobody attacks.
Kevin files. Rita publishes. The attack becomes evidence in both channels.

### 5.2 — Retaliatory Filing Classification (unnamed)

Not detection — classification. The temporal proximity of the adversary's filing
to the operator's filing automatically classifies it as retaliatory.
The operator doesn't need to prove intent. The clock proves timing.

**Trigger:** Shayla's retaliatory PPO filed within days of Kevin's complaint
**Maps to:** CROSS_PROTECTION §Retaliatory Filing Detection
**Naming seed:** Same as 1.4 — the clock. Maybe these are the same capability.

### 5.3 — Information Quarantine (unnamed)

After the filing blast, the adversary network cannot communicate without risk.
Any contact between defendants is potentially discoverable as enterprise coordination.
Any attorney hired by one defendant may have conflicts with another. The network
is quarantined by the filings themselves.

**Trigger:** Zero coordination visible across 5 defendants after 21 days
**Maps to:** fEAR §Adversarial — "the adversary is in a bind"
**Naming seed:** The filings are the fence. The silence proves it's working.
