# guerillaGorilla — Primal Definition

**The legal primal.** Legal existence as infrastructure.
**Parent framework:** K-NOME (constrained optimization methodology)
**Companion primals:** squirrel (AI compute), barraCuda (GPU), wetSpring (bio)
**Operational loops:** fEAR (sensing) + STRIDe (motion)

---

## DEFINITION

guerillaGorilla is a K-NOME primal that instantiates constrained optimization
in the domain of **legal existence** — the complete set of systems, documents,
processes, rights, and institutional interfaces that determine whether a person
can function formally in a society designed to be navigated by professionals.

It is not a litigation tool. Litigation is one output. The primal covers:

- **Identity** — the documents that prove you exist (birth cert, SSN, DD-214,
  drivers license, passport)
- **Rights** — the legal frameworks that protect you (parental rights, custody,
  protective orders, civil rights)
- **Finance** — the systems that determine your economic existence (bank accounts,
  credit, benefits, taxes, employment records)
- **Protection** — the apparatus that shields you from harm (PPOs, restraining
  orders, emergency orders, criminal complaints)
- **Navigation** — the process of moving through institutional systems (e-filing,
  agency complaints, form completion, deadlines, service requirements)
- **Accountability** — the adversarial response system that holds others to account
  (civil suits, regulatory complaints, OSINT, discovery, subpoenas)
- **Formalization** — the conversion of informal exploitation into documented,
  enforceable claims (the fEAR informal→formal inversion)

Each of these is a **subsystem** of the primal, the way barraCuda has subsystems
for shader dispatch, precision management, and cross-vendor abstraction.

ecoPrimals names things that are **foundational infrastructure** — capabilities

- barraCuda is foundational because without GPU compute, no physics runs
- wetSpring is foundational because without sequencing pipelines, no genomics
- songBird is foundational because without networking, no mesh
- NestGate is foundational because without storage, no data

**guerillaGorilla is foundational because without legal existence, nothing
else matters.**

DaSean Mitchell had no custody order for 9 years. Without that formal legal
existence as a parent, he had:
- No right to access his children's school records
- No right to determine where they lived
- No standing to challenge Shayla's unilateral decisions
- No protection when his identity documents were locked away

He existed informally. Informality was the weapon used against him. The
primal that converts informal existence into formal existence — that gives
a person the legal infrastructure to function — is as foundational as the
primal that converts electrical signals into GPU compute.

## ARCHITECTURE

### Inputs

guerillaGorilla consumes:

| Input type | Sources | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Public records** | PACER, ROD, LARA, SOS, OTIS, court dockets | Bankruptcy filings, deeds, entity filings, campaign finance |
| **Adversary actions** | Court filings, sworn exhibits, social media, communications | Shayla's 192 pages, Banks' LinkedIn, "I'm a lawyer" |
| **Institutional data** | FOIA responses, charter contracts, audit reports, board minutes | PCA-DPSCD contract, MacDowell FY2025 audit |
| **Legal framework** | Statutes, court rules, case law, administrative codes | MCR 2.305, 18 USC 1962, MCL 15.181 |
| **Operator intelligence** | Pattern recognition, human psychology, relationship knowledge | "That's her grandad's car" — context no database holds |

### Processing

guerillaGorilla processes inputs through two concurrent loops:

```
fEAR (exteroception — what is the adversary doing?)
  flow → Evolution → Adversarial → Response
  Sensing → case mutation → adversary as fuel → calibrated output

STRIDe (proprioception + locomotion — where are WE and how do we move?)
  Strategy → Tactics → Reconnaissance → Intelligence → Deployment → execution
  Where we're going → how → what we see → what we know → what we stage → step
```

The R⇄I loop within STRIDe is the neural feedback layer — continuous
bidirectional exchange between sensing and understanding. It tells the
system where it is relative to reality.

squirrel provides the AI compute substrate for both loops — statute lookup,
document drafting, cross-referencing, strategic modeling, reaction profiling.

### Outputs

guerillaGorilla produces:

| Output type | Examples | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal filings** | Complaints, motions, amendments, PPOs, subpoenas | Filed and accepted by court (exit 0) |
| **Regulatory complaints** | JTC, State Bar, MDE, AG, DPSCD, MDHHS | Agency acknowledgment/case number |
| **Evidence packages** | OSINT briefs, network maps, exhibit indices | Sourced, cited, reproducible by anyone |
| **Identity documents** | Custody orders, authorized rep letters, vital record requests | Institutional acceptance |
| **Protective orders** | PPOs, emergency custody, no-contact orders | Court issuance |
| **Discovery instruments** | Interrogatories, document requests, subpoenas | Served and enforceable |
| **Strategic analysis** | OPFOR profiles, reaction models, timeline projections | Validated against observed adversary behavior |

### Validation Criteria

Like all ecoPrimals outputs, guerillaGorilla artifacts must be:

1. **Sourced** — every claim traced to a primary record (PACER case #, ROD doc #,
   LARA ID, statute citation)
2. **Reproducible** — anyone with the same inputs (public records) reaches the same
   conclusions
3. **Self-verifying** — the artifact carries its own proof (exhibit index maps to
   source documents, network map links to primary records)
4. **Provenance-traced** — chain of custody from raw source to finished artifact
   (when downloaded, how processed, by whom)

The test is the same as for any baseCamp paper: if the published result and the
reproduction agree, reality is confirmed. If they disagree, someone is wrong,
and the methodology determines who.

Banks published: "J.D., Ph.D., licensed attorney."
guerillaGorilla reproduced: State Bar of Michigan → zero results.
Discrepancy with known resolution.

## WHAT GUERILLAGORILLA IS NOT

### Not a litigation-only tool

Litigation is one output channel. Most of what guerillaGorilla does is
pre-litigation infrastructure:
- Helping DaSean understand his parental rights
- Walking him through e-filing for emergency custody
- Identifying which forms (CC 375, 376, 380, 381) apply to which PPO type
- Preparing subpoenas for future service
- Recovering identity documents from a hostile household

A person who never files a lawsuit still needs guerillaGorilla for their
birth certificate, their custody order, their benefits, their tax filings.

### Not an automation platform (yet)

Currently, guerillaGorilla is a methodology — a structured way of thinking
about legal existence that the operator executes with AI assistance. In time,
subsystems will automate:
- PACER API for batch case searches
- FOIA template library for common requests
- ROD scripted pulls with auto-extraction
- Filing workflow checklists with deadline tracking
- Evidence database with cross-reference indexing

But the methodology is the primal. The automation is the optimization. You
don't need barraCuda to do physics — you can run CPU. But barraCuda makes
it 100× faster. guerillaGorilla without automation still works. Automation
makes it walk faster.

### Not a solo-only system

guerillaGorilla was designed for a single operator, but the primal is named
for a **social animal** for a reason. Cross-protection between operators
(Mode 2: mutualism) and network sensing across multiple operators (Mode 3)
are not add-ons — they are the natural extension of the troop structure
that keeps gorillas alive. See `CROSS_PROTECTION.md` for the full framework
on proactive defense, Mutual Assured Documentation, and retaliatory filing
detection.

### Not attorney replacement

guerillaGorilla does not replace lawyers. It replaces the **infrastructure
that makes lawyers expensive**: the research, the document preparation, the
cross-referencing, the network mapping, the evidence organization, the
strategic modeling. The lawyer provides courtroom presence, deposition skills,
bar card, and professional judgment. guerillaGorilla provides everything else.

When a firm picks up a guerillaGorilla-prepared case, they inherit:
- A built evidence package
- A mapped network with primary sources
- Pre-drafted filings
- Cross-referenced exhibits
- Strategic analysis with reaction profiles
- A plaintiff who can continue as a paid research consultant

The handoff is not "here's my mess, please help." The handoff is "here's a
turnkey case. You file it, you litigate it, I keep feeding it data."

## THE THEORETICAL SPACE

guerillaGorilla exists in a space that didn't exist before AI:

| Era | Legal capability for non-attorneys |
|---|---|
| Pre-internet | Essentially zero. Law libraries physical. Forms paper-only. |
| Internet (1995-2015) | Google searches. LegalZoom forms. Self-help books. |
| Legal tech (2015-2024) | Document automation. E-filing. Online court access. |
| **AI-augmented (2024-present)** | **Synthetic legal intelligence. Statute recall. Document drafting. Cross-referencing at machine speed. Strategic modeling. OSINT at scale.** |

guerillaGorilla is the first primal designed for the AI-augmented era. It
assumes the operator has access to synthetic intelligence (squirrel) and builds
the methodology around what that enables:

- **Comprehensive legal knowledge** without law school
- **Document drafting** without a paralegal
- **Cross-referencing** without Westlaw/LexisNexis
- **Strategic modeling** without a litigation consultant
- **OSINT at scale** without a private investigator

The operator provides direction, pattern recognition, human psychology,
and execution. squirrel provides the legal knowledge substrate. guerillaGorilla
is the primal that composes them into a functioning legal existence apparatus.
