## THE BINARY TRAP: THE FRAUD IS PRESENT TENSE

Barry County's docket states:

> "RE: Defendant's Request For Status Conference Is Dismissed;
> **Sent to Defendant via last known address.**"

That statement is either true or false. There is no middle ground.

### PATH A: They used USPS

If Barry County mailed the order through the United States Postal
Service, then:

1. USPS accepted the item into its system
2. USPS transported it to the destination facility
3. MDOC's mailroom logged incoming mail upon arrival

**MDOC FOIA shows no record of arrival.**

Michigan prisons log all incoming mail — including mail processed
through digitization vendors like TextBehind. If USPS carried this
document, the mailroom would have a record. There is none.

Therefore: either USPS never received the item (the court lied
about mailing it), or USPS lost it (and the court has no proof
it was ever sent — no tracking, no postage record, no certificate
of mailing).

**Either way, the docket entry "Sent to Defendant" is a false
statement in an official record. Right now. Today.**

If USPS was cited as the carrier and USPS never carried it, that
is 18 USC 1341 — mail fraud. The court used the claimed authority
of the federal mail system to create the appearance of compliance
with MCR 2.107(C)(3). The mails were not actually used; they were
invoked to legitimize a false record.

### PATH B: They did NOT use USPS

If Barry County did not mail the order through USPS — if it was
hand-delivered, sent by courier, or simply never sent at all —
then it is **not mail**. It is a flier. An internal document.
A piece of paper that never entered any independent delivery
system.

In that case:

1. There is NO independent third party who touched the document
2. There is NO tracking, no postage, no delivery confirmation
3. The county is **wholly and solely responsible** for proving
   the document moved from the courthouse to the defendant
4. The county cannot prove this because no verification system
   exists outside the county's own records

**The docket says "Sent to Defendant via last known address."
If there is no USPS involvement, Barry County is the only
entity claiming it was sent. The county is verifying itself.
That is not verification. That is an assertion.**

A court record that states a document was sent, when the sending
cannot be independently verified, is **functionally perjury by
the state**. MCL 750.248 (falsifying a public record) and MCL
750.423 (perjury) do not require the statement to be proven
false — they require that the statement, as entered into an
official record, be verifiably true. If it cannot be verified,
it cannot be relied upon, and the entry is a false public record.

### THE TRAP IS CLOSED

| Path | Claim | Evidence | Result |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| A: USPS used | "Sent via USPS" | MDOC FOIA: nothing arrived | **Mail fraud (18 USC 1341)** — USPS cited as carrier with no proof of carriage |
| B: USPS not used | "Sent to Defendant" | No independent carrier exists | **Perjury / False record (MCL 750.248/423)** — county verifying itself with no external validation |

**There is no Path C.** Either the federal mail system was used or
it was not. Both paths terminate in fraud. The only escape is
producing the verification — which is exactly what we demand.

## THE VERIFICATION DEMAND

Barry County must produce ALL of the following for Entry 135:

### Electronic Record (MiCOURT)
1. **Creation timestamp** — exact date/time the entry was created
   in the database (not the printed "event date")
2. **Modification history** — every edit, including timestamp and
   nature of change
3. **User account** — which clerk/judge account created the entry
4. **Public visibility timestamp** — when the entry became visible
   on the public docket

### Mailing Record
5. **Postage record** — USPS receipt, meter stamp, certificate of
   mailing, or bulk mailing log
6. **Address used** — the specific address (MCR 2.107(C)(3)
   requires the address in the party's pleadings, not "last known")
7. **Delivery confirmation** — any proof the document reached the
   destination, or reached USPS for transport
8. **MDOC intake record** — corresponding entry in the prison
   mailroom log showing receipt

### Why This Is Non-Negotiable

MiCOURT is a database. Every database built after 1990 generates
audit trail metadata automatically. This data exists. The question
is whether Barry County will produce it or suppress it.

If they produce it and the creation timestamp is September 10 →
the entry is verified. Case closed on that point.

If they produce it and the creation timestamp is September 11 or
12 → the entry was backdated. That is a falsified public record.

If they refuse to produce it → the entry cannot be authenticated.
An unauthenticated entry in a court record is a chain of custody
violation. The court cannot ask litigants and appellate courts to
rely on records whose own provenance is unverifiable.

## DATA SCIENCE EXPERT FRAMEWORK

### The Provenance Principle (Kevin Mok, M.S. Data Science, MSU)

In any forensic discipline — laboratory science, military
intelligence, digital forensics, data science — a document
without provenance cannot be authenticated. Provenance requires:

1. **Creation timestamp** — when was the record actually created?
2. **Modification history** — was it changed after creation?
3. **User attribution** — which account created/modified it?
4. **Independent verification** — can a third party confirm the
   record's journey from origin to destination?

**If any of these four elements is missing, the record fails
authentication.** This is not a standard I invented. It is the
standard applied in every federal laboratory, every military
intelligence shop, every digital forensics examination, and
every peer-reviewed scientific publication.

### The Lab Standard

In a BSL2 laboratory, if a technician reports a result but cannot
produce the chain of custody — when the sample was collected, who
handled it, whether it was contaminated, whether the result can
be reproduced — the result is **rejected**. Not questioned.
Rejected. Not because we know it is wrong, but because we cannot
know it is right.

A court docket entry is a forensic artifact. It is subject to
the same authentication requirements as any other record that
determines outcomes for human beings. If the metadata does not
corroborate the printed date, the printed date is not a fact.
It is a claim. And a claim entered into an official record
without verification is a false statement.

## FEDERAL STATUTES

### 18 USC 1341 — Mail Fraud (PATH A)

**Elements:** (1) Scheme to defraud; (2) Use of the mails in
furtherance.

**Application:** The docket states mail was sent. MDOC FOIA
confirms nothing arrived. If USPS was cited as carrier and USPS
never carried the document, the court invoked the federal mail
system to legitimize a false record. Each such entry is a
separate count.

### 18 USC 1343 — Wire Fraud (BOTH PATHS)

**Elements:** (1) Scheme to defraud; (2) Use of interstate wires
in furtherance.

**Application:** MiCOURT operates over interstate
telecommunications infrastructure. Creating a backdated entry in
MiCOURT IS the use of interstate wires. If Entry 135 was created
on Sep 11 or 12 but dated Sep 10, the electronic system was the
instrument of fraud. Each backdated entry is a separate count.

### 18 USC 1346 — Honest Services Fraud (BOTH PATHS)

**Application:** Judge Schipper is under JTC review. Authorizing
or permitting backdated entries while under JTC review deprives
litigants of the honest judicial services they are owed. The
scheme: maintain the appearance of compliant judicial
administration while failing to serve defendants and covering
the gap with fabricated records.

### MCL 750.248 — False Public Record (BOTH PATHS)

An entry in the register of actions stating "Sent to Defendant
via last known address" when the sending cannot be verified is a
false entry in a public record. The falsity is not that the order
is wrong — it is that the record of service is unverifiable and
therefore unreliable. An unreliable entry in an official record
maintained under MCR 8.119 is a falsified public record.

### MCL 750.423 — Perjury (PATH B)

If the court did not use USPS and has no independent verification
of delivery, the statement "Sent to Defendant" is a false
assertion by a state actor entered into an official proceeding
record. That is perjury.
