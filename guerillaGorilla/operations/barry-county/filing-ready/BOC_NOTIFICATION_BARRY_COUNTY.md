Members of the Barry County Board of Commissioners:

I am writing to inform the Board that I have independently
reviewed publicly available records and published reporting
concerning the Barry County Trial Court's record-keeping and
service practices.

I am a Michigan citizen with a Master of Science in Data
Science from Michigan State University, a Bachelor of Science
in Microbiology, and U.S. Army service. I have no personal
stake in any Barry County case and am not affiliated with any
party in any pending matter.

Based on my professional assessment of the documented
evidence — including the amicus curiae brief filed by Rita
Williams in *In re Foley*, Michigan Supreme Court Case No.
170670, and her investigative reporting at Clutch Justice —
I have filed notifications with the following authorities
during the week of September 15, 2026:

1. Formal demand to Barry County Court Administration for
   MiCOURT audit trail records
2. U.S. Postal Service Office of Inspector General
3. U.S. Postal Inspection Service
4. Michigan Judicial Tenure Commission (re: Judge Schipper
   and Chief Judge Doherty)
5. FBI Detroit Public Corruption Unit
6. State Court Administrative Office (MCR 8.113)
7. Michigan Attorney General

I am not asking the Board to take any specific action. I am
informing you that a third independent party — in addition to
Jeffrey Snowden (who addressed the Board in February 2026) and
Ms. Williams (who has been reporting on these issues since
2023) — has now independently assessed the documented evidence
and concluded that it warrants formal review by multiple state
and federal oversight authorities.

The Board is now on notice from three independent sources that
the trial court it funds and houses has documented record
integrity concerns spanning multiple years, multiple judges,
and multiple cases.

I am available to discuss this further if the Board wishes.
