**Type of crime:** Mail Fraud

Barry County Circuit Court

**Describe what happened:**

Barry County Circuit Court has repeatedly entered statements in
official court records claiming that court documents were mailed
to parties via the United States Postal Service, when independent
evidence demonstrates no such mailings were received.

I am a Michigan citizen with an M.S. in Data Science and B.S. in
Microbiology from Michigan State University and three years of
U.S. Army intelligence service. I am reporting this independently
based on publicly documented evidence.

INCIDENT 1: On August 11, 2026, Barry County entered an order in
Case No. 2022-0000000523-FH and claimed it was "mailed by first-
class mail." A certified Michigan Department of Corrections FOIA
response shows no record of that order ever reaching the
incarcerated defendant.

INCIDENT 2: On or around September 10-12, 2026, Docket Entry
No. 135 in a separate Barry County matter appeared on the public
docket stating: "Sent to Defendant via last known address." An
MDOC FOIA response shows no mail arrived.

INCIDENT 3: On July 1, 2026, USPS delivered filings to Barry
County Circuit Court (tracking 9205590324992801759012, confirmed
delivered). The court's docket initially reflected these filings.
On July 8, the docket entries DISAPPEARED. USPS delivered but
the court erased the record of receipt.

Additionally, USPS tracking 9205590324992801759753 confirms
delivery to the Barry County Prosecutor's Office on July 2, 2026.

Either Barry County deposited these documents with USPS and they
were lost (a USPS matter), or Barry County never deposited them
and falsely claimed USPS involvement in official court records
(mail fraud under 18 USC 1341).

This has been independently documented by journalist Rita
Williams of Clutch Justice in her amicus curiae brief filed with
the Michigan Supreme Court in In re Foley, Case No. 170670, on
September 11, 2026.

I have separately filed a complaint with the USPS Office of
Inspector General and a demand for audit trail records with Barry
County Court Administration.

Note: Mark Foley, the direct victim in the underlying case, has
previously contacted USPS Postal Inspection regarding these same
issues. I am an independent complainant corroborating his report.

**Dollar amount of loss:** N/A — this is not a financial fraud
but a deprivation of civil rights using false claims of USPS
