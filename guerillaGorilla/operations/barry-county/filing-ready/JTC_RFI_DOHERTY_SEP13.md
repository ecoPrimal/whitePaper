## ALLEGATIONS

### 1. Administrative Suppression of a Litigant's Access to the Court

On November 23, 2022, Chief Judge Doherty issued an order
designating plaintiff Mark Foley as a "vexatious filer" in
Case No. 2020-656-DM. This order:

(a) Restricted Foley to four filings per year;

(b) Required all filings to be submitted by mail only — the
same delivery channel that had already been shown to be
compromised (mail repeatedly sent to wrong addresses despite
two documented corrections);

(c) Prohibited court staff from responding to Foley's emails,
eliminating his only reliable method of communication with
the court;

(d) Was subsequently submitted by opposing counsel Brad Gee to
the Attorney Grievance Commission to shut down Foley's
pending ethics complaint against Gee — effectively weaponizing
a judicial order to obstruct an independent administrative
remedy.

These are administrative actions — controlling staff behavior,
managing filings, and governing court access. They are not
judicial rulings on the merits of any dispute.

### 2. Failure to Supervise Court Staff — Record Tampering

Chief Judge Doherty bears administrative responsibility for the
proper functioning of the Barry County Trial Court. Under his
supervision:

(a) Chief Probation Officer Chrystal Lambert physically altered
an officially stamped court filing by applying correction fluid
(White-Out) to the received stamp before returning the document.
The filing never reached the Register of Actions. This matter
is the subject of a pending SCAO investigation initiated in
February 2026.

(b) Docket entries in Case No. 2022-0000000523-FH disappeared
from the public register of actions on July 8, 2026, despite
USPS delivery confirmation (tracking nos. 9205590324992801759012
and 9205590324992801759753) proving the underlying filings were
delivered to the court.

(c) An order in the same case, entered August 11, 2026, was
represented in the docket as mailed by first-class mail. A
certified MDOC records production shows no record of that order
ever reaching the defendant.

(d) Docket Entry No. 135 in a separate Barry County matter
appeared on the public docket between 3:42 PM on September 11,
2026, and approximately 9:49 AM on September 12, 2026, carrying
a backdated event date of September 10.

As Chief Judge, Judge Doherty is responsible for court
administration, including the integrity of the court's
electronic case management system (MiCOURT) and the supervision
of court staff who create, modify, and maintain docket entries.
These are administrative duties, not judicial acts.

### 3. Technology Governance Failure

Judge Doherty serves on the State Courts Technology
Implementation Committee — the body responsible for court
technology infrastructure across Michigan. Despite this role,
the court he administers produces electronic records whose
integrity cannot be independently verified:

(a) MiCOURT operates on IBM Db2, which generates audit trail
metadata automatically (creation timestamps, modification
history, user accounts). SCAO audit standards mandate
modification reports and separation of duties. Whether these
controls are functioning in Barry County has not been
demonstrated.

(b) I have submitted a formal demand to Barry County Court
Administration for the audit trail records for Entry No. 135.
The response to that demand will establish whether the system
Judge Doherty oversees is producing verifiable records.

A chief judge who sits on the statewide technology committee
while administering a court whose technology produces
unverifiable records is not fulfilling the administrative
duties of the office.

### 4. Pattern of Administrative Failure

The above incidents are documented by independent investigative
journalist Rita Williams (Clutch Justice) in her amicus curiae
brief filed September 11, 2026, in *In re Foley*, Michigan
Supreme Court Case No. 170670, in which Judge Doherty is a
named defendant. Ms. Williams has documented this pattern across
multiple articles spanning December 2025 through September 2026.

I am filing independently of Ms. Williams. I have no affiliation
with her or with any party in the matters she has documented.

## REQUEST

I respectfully request that this Commission investigate whether
Chief Judge Doherty's administrative actions and supervisory
failures constitute conduct prejudicial to the proper
administration of justice.
