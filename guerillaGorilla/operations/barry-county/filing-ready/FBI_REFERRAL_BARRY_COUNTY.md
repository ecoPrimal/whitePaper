## STREAM 1: WIRE FRAUD AND MAIL FRAUD

### The Pattern (5 documented incidents, 3 years)

Independent investigative journalist Rita Williams, editor of
Clutch Justice, has documented the following pattern in Barry
County's court records:

**1. Persistent service failure (2022-2026):** In Case No.
2020-656-DM (*In re Foley*), plaintiff Mark Foley's correct
mailing address was placed on the court's record twice — by
deputy clerk letter on October 3, 2022, and by a Friend of the
Court referee on June 16, 2023. Mail continued going to the
wrong address after each correction.

**2. Physical record tampering (February 2026):** A court filing
had its received stamp obscured with correction fluid (White-Out)
by Chief Probation Officer Chrystal Lambert before being returned
without reaching the Register of Actions. This is the subject of
a pending SCAO investigation.

**3. Disappearing docket entries (July 2026):** In Case No.
2022-0000000523-FH, USPS delivery confirmation (tracking numbers
9205590324992801759012 and 9205590324992801759753) proves an
incarcerated litigant's motions were delivered to the court on
July 1, 2026, and to the Prosecutor's Office on July 2. The
docket initially reflected these motions as filed. On July 8,
2026 — the same day Ms. Williams filed correspondence with the
Michigan Supreme Court about prison mail delivery — the docket
entries disappeared. They did not reappear until August 7.

**4. False mailing claim (August 2026):** On August 11, 2026,
the court denied the motions and represented in its docket that
the order was mailed by first-class mail. A certified MDOC FOIA
response shows no record of that order ever reaching the
defendant.

**5. Backdated docket entry (September 2026):** Docket Entry
No. 135 in a separate Barry County matter was confirmed absent
from the public docket at 3:42 PM on September 11, 2026 — seven
minutes after the Michigan Supreme Court accepted Ms. Williams'
amicus brief challenging Barry County's record-keeping. By
approximately 9:49 AM on September 12, the entry had appeared,
carrying a backdated event date of September 10 and claiming the
order was "Sent to Defendant via last known address." MDOC FOIA
shows no mail arrival.

### Federal Statutes Implicated

**18 USC 1341 — Mail Fraud:** Barry County's docket claims
documents were sent via United States mail. MDOC's certified
records show no arrival. Either USPS carried documents that
never arrived (and the court has no postage documentation) or
the court falsely cited USPS as the carrier — invoking the
federal mail system to legitimize false records.

**18 USC 1343 — Wire Fraud:** Barry County's MiCOURT case
management system operates on IBM Db2 over interstate
telecommunications infrastructure (with data replicated to
Microsoft Azure SQL). Creating backdated entries in MiCOURT is
the use of interstate wires to create false records. Each
backdated entry is a separate count.

**18 USC 1346 — Honest Services Fraud:** Judge Michael Schipper,
named on Entry No. 135, is currently under review by the
Michigan Judicial Tenure Commission (Request for Investigation
No. 2026-27270, assigned to staff review August 11, 2026).
Authorizing or permitting backdated entries while under JTC
review deprives litigants of honest judicial services.

### Technical Note

MiCOURT's Db2 database generates audit trail metadata
automatically: creation timestamps, modification history, and
user account data. This data exists. A subpoena to SCAO (which
administers MiCOURT statewide) or to Microsoft (which hosts the
Azure SQL replica) would independently verify or refute every
contested date. Barry County controls the Db2 layer directly
but does not control the Azure replication timestamps, the
Judicial Data Warehouse ingestion records, the network
authentication logs, or the database backups — all of which
are held by SCAO or Microsoft.

## STREAM 2: CIVIL RIGHTS VIOLATIONS

### Retaliation Against Journalist

Each time Ms. Williams took an oversight action, the court
responded with record manipulation:

- February 2026: Her filing's received stamp was physically
  destroyed with White-Out by Chief Probation Officer Lambert.

- July 8, 2026: The day she filed MSC correspondence about
  prison mail delivery, docket entries in the case she was
  monitoring disappeared.

- September 11-12, 2026: Within 18 hours of the MSC accepting
  her amicus brief, a backdated entry appeared on a docket she
  monitors.

### Retaliation Against Litigant

Mr. Foley's access to the court was systematically eliminated:

- October 2022: Chief Judge Doherty denied subpoenas before
  Foley's disqualification hearing could begin, denied a
  continuance despite a pending Court of Appeals complaint, and
  denied the disqualification motion.

- November 2022: Doherty issued a "vexatious filer" order
  restricting Foley to four filings per year by mail only —
  the same delivery channel already shown to be compromised.
  Court staff were prohibited from responding to Foley's emails.
  Opposing counsel Brad Gee submitted this order to the Attorney
  Grievance Commission to kill Foley's pending ethics complaint.

- July-August 2026: Foley's motions delivered via USPS were
  deleted from the docket, then denied, then claimed to be
  mailed — with MDOC confirming nothing arrived.

- August 17, 2026: Foley was arrested. August 19: arraigned.
  On orders he alleges he was never properly served.

### Federal Statutes Implicated

**18 USC 241 — Conspiracy Against Rights:** The suppression
architecture requires coordination among multiple actors —
judge, opposing counsel, probation officer — to restrict,
silence, and then deprive a litigant of access to courts and
due process.

**18 USC 242 — Deprivation of Rights Under Color of Law:**
Judges and court officers acting under color of state law
willfully deprived Foley of his right to due process (14th
Amendment), access to courts, and right to petition (1st
Amendment). Enhanced penalties apply because the deprivation
resulted in bodily restraint (arrest and arraignment).

**First Amendment Retaliation:** Both Foley's petitioning
(filings, complaints) and Williams' journalism (publications,
amicus briefs) are protected First Amendment activity. The
temporal correlation between oversight actions and retaliatory
record manipulation establishes the retaliatory pattern.
