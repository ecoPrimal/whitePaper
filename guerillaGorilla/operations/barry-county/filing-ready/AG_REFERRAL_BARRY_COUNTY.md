I am a Michigan citizen with a Master of Science in Data

None of the actions described below constitute challenges to
judicial decisions or rulings. The actions at issue —
creating and dating electronic records, mailing or failing
to mail court documents, and supervising court staff — are
administrative and ministerial acts outside the scope of
judicial immunity under *Forrester v. White*, 484 U.S. 219
(1988).

## THE FALSE RECORDS

Barry County Circuit Court's register of actions, maintained
under MCR 8.119, currently contains entries stating that court
documents were "sent to defendant" when independent evidence
establishes no such delivery occurred:

1. **August 11, 2026:** An order in Case No.
   2022-0000000523-FH was entered and the docket represents it
   was mailed by first-class mail. A certified Michigan
   Department of Corrections FOIA response shows no record of
   the order ever reaching the incarcerated defendant.

2. **September 10-12, 2026:** Docket Entry No. 135 in a
   separate Barry County matter states "Sent to Defendant via
   last known address." The entry was confirmed absent from the
   public docket at 3:42 PM on September 11 and present by
   approximately 9:49 AM on September 12, carrying a backdated
   date of September 10. MDOC FOIA shows no mail arrival.

Under MCL 750.248, any person who makes, alters, forges, or
counterfeits a public record, or who knowingly places false
information in a public record, is guilty of a felony. The
register of actions is a public record. An entry claiming a
document was mailed when no evidence of mailing exists, and
when the receiving facility's certified records show no
arrival, is a false entry in a public record.

## THE BROADER PATTERN

These are not isolated incidents. Independent investigative
journalist Rita Williams has documented in her amicus curiae
brief in *In re Foley*, Michigan Supreme Court Case No.
170670 (enclosed):

- Docket entries that disappeared and later reappeared
  (July-August 2026)
- An officially stamped court filing whose received stamp was
  obscured with correction fluid by Chief Probation Officer
  Chrystal Lambert (February 2026, SCAO investigation pending)
- Persistent service of court documents to wrong addresses
  despite documented corrections (2022-2026)
- A litigant who was arrested on orders he alleges he was never
  properly served (August 17, 2026)

Every judge in Barry County's 5th Circuit courthouse has been
the subject of investigation by the Judicial Tenure Commission,
the Michigan Supreme Court, or both.
