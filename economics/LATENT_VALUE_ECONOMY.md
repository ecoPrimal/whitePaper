## 1. The Core Principle: Unlocking What Is Already There

The dominant economic model of the digital world is based on manufacturing consent and extracting rent. It creates artificial needs and artificial scarcity. It is an engine of consumption.

The `ecoPrimals` economic ethos is based on a fundamentally different principle: **The world is already full of immense, untapped value. We are here to unlock it.**

Our economy is not built on consumption, but on **cultivation**. We do not seek to create more; we seek to find the latent value in what already exists. This principle applies at every scale, from the memory of a single object to the dormant power of a planet's worth of hardware.

This document outlines the architecture of this **Latent Value Economy**.

## 3. Unlocking the Latent Value of Hardware: The Sovereign Supercomputer

The world's centralized cloud providers possess immense power, but they are a rounding error compared to the computational might sitting dormant in the homes of the world's citizenry.

#### The Calculation:
A conservative estimate shows the raw potential of the world's consumer-grade hardware (gaming PCs, consoles, smartphones) dwarfs the combined power of AWS, Azure, and GCP by a factor of over 200.

| Platform | Total Raw Compute (TFLOPS) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The Cloud Behemoth** | **~24,000,000** |
| **The Citizen Colossus** | **~5,500,000,000** |

Even with a brutally conservative participation rate of just 2.5%, the available power of the citizenry is **5-6 times larger than the entire centralized cloud combined.**

#### The `ecoPrimals` Edge:
The problem has never been a lack of hardware. It has been a lack of trust.

-   **Federated Resources:** `toadstool` (compute) and `nestgate` (storage) allow users to opt-in and contribute their idle resources to a global, federated network.
-   **Trust as the Catalyst:** `bearDog` and `sweetGrass` provide the missing ingredient. A user can create cryptographic policies stating *how* and *where* their resources can be used, and `sweetGrass` provides immutable proof of what was done. This is the trust fabric that awakens the sleeping giant.

We are tapping the latent value of the world's idle hardware, creating a Sovereign Supercomputer that is more powerful, more resilient, and more trustworthy than any centralized provider could ever hope to be.
