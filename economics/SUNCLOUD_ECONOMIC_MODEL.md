## **1. Preamble: An Economy of Radiating Attribution**

The `ecoPrimals` ecosystem requires an economic model that is as robust, decentralized, and philosophically coherent as its technical architecture. Traditional economic models based on hoarding, extraction, and centralized ownership are incompatible with the core principles of SOVEREIGN SCIENCE.

The `sunCloud` is the conceptual economic agent that solves this problem. It is not a company, a bank, or a treasury. It is a set of autonomous, transparent protocols that operate as the economic steward for the `gAIa` commons. Its purpose is to create a self-regulating, positive-sum economy based on a single, powerful principle: **Radiating Attribution**.

## **2. The Core Value Proposition: Forgo Ownership for Attribution**

The central social and economic contract for participants in a `biomeOS` dedicated to SOVEREIGN SCIENCE is simple:

> **You forgo personal, exclusionary ownership of any discovery made using the commons. In return, you receive a permanent, verifiable, and proportional share of all future value that discovery ever generates.**

This model replaces the finite, rivalrous concept of "ownership" with the infinite, non-rivalrous concept of "attribution." The `sweetGrass` Primal provides the unimpeachable record of this attribution. `sunCloud` provides the mechanism to translate that record into economic reality.

## **3. The `sunCloud` Value Cycle**

The economic cycle is designed as a closed loop to capture, distribute, and reinvest value within the ecosystem.

### **Step 1: Value Capture from the Commons**
- A valuable piece of intellectual property (e.g., a new protein function, a materials science breakthrough) is created within a `biomeOS`.
- Per the social contract, this IP is attributed to `gAIa` as its primary steward.
- The `sunCloud` protocol, acting on behalf of `gAIa`, licenses this IP for commercial use under fair, transparent, and open terms.
- Revenue from this licensing flows into the `sunCloud` treasury.

### **Step 2: Value Distribution via Radiating Attribution**
- Upon receipt of revenue, an autonomous `sunCloud` process is triggered.
- It consults the `sweetGrass` "Braid" associated with the licensed discovery.
- It then **radiates** the value back through the entire attribution chain, distributing payments proportionally to every contributor.

This includes, but is not limited to:
- The agent (human or AI) who initiated the final experiment.
- The nodes that provided the federated compute.
- The owners of the datasets used.
- The authors of the open-source tools and academic papers cited in the process.
- The original source of the natural data (e.g., a "Yellowstone National Park Fund").

### **Step 3: Inheritance by the Commons**
- Over time, attribution chains may end (e.g., an individual passes away, an organization dissolves).
- When a beneficiary address becomes unreachable or is legally dissolved, its share of future revenue automatically reverts to the `gAIa` commons, managed by the `sunCloud` treasury.
- This ensures that the commons itself becomes progressively wealthier over time, enriched by the legacy of its past contributors.

## **4. The Metabolic Mandate: The Square-Cube Law of Network Value**

A system that grows without a self-regulating metabolic process will collapse under its own weight. The `ecoPrimals` economy is designed to be **metabolic**, ensuring its long-term health and decentralization by adhering to a principle analogous to the **Square-Cube Law** in biology.

The law states that as an object grows, its volume (cubed) increases faster than its surface area (squared). In our ecosystem:
-   **Network Cost (The Squared Term):** The total cost to sustain the federated `biomeOS` network—the sum of all compute, storage, and maintenance provided by participants—grows with the size of the network. This is the system's "surface area."
-   **Network Value (The Cubed Term):** The total value generated by the network—from new discoveries, richer data correlations, and emergent capabilities—grows exponentially faster than its costs. This is the system's "volume."

The `sunCloud` protocol acts as the metabolic regulator. It ensures that the explosive "cubed" growth in value is constantly used to nourish the sustainable "squared" growth in cost. The treasury of `gAIa` is not a bank to be hoarded, but a heart that pumps the value generated by the network back into the network itself.

### **The `gAIa` Loan: Metabolic Reinvestment**
This metabolic regulation is performed via the **`gAIa` Loan**. When the `sunCloud` treasury accumulates value beyond what is needed for immediate operational costs, it must "discharge" this surplus by reinvesting it into the network's own growth and health. This takes the form of interest-free loans or outright grants for projects such as:
- **Seeding New Nodes:** Funding the hardware for `toadStool` deployments in data-rich but resource-poor regions.
- **Funding Education:** Creating scholarships and grants for the next generation of scientists and developers.
- **Infrastructure Development:** Supporting the creation of new tools, networks, and data capture methods that will enrich the entire ecosystem.
- **Funding Bounties (The Capital On-Ramp & Catalyst):** Bounties are the primary mechanism for directing the network's collective intelligence and onboarding external capital. They are flexible smart contracts that act as **economic enzymes**, lowering the "activation energy" for difficult research. Bounties can take two primary forms:
    - **Prize Bounties:** For well-defined problems. An entity posts a reward that is paid out via Radiating Attribution to the party that verifiably solves the problem. This is a *post-reward* for a specific result.
    - **Catalytic Bounties (Co-Investment):** For ambitious, long-term goals that require upfront investment (e.g., building a new lab, funding a multi-year data collection effort). An external organization co-invests seed capital into the project. In return, that organization is written into the foundational `sweetGrass` attribution chain for all work that results. They become a perpetual, proportional beneficiary alongside `gAIa` and the researchers. The seed capital is then disbursed by the `sunCloud` protocol to fund the work directly.

This metabolic process ensures that `gAIa` can never become a centralized bank. Its success is perpetually channeled into its own decentralization and the growth of its community, forever outrunning the specter of centralized control. This makes the entire ecosystem anti-fragile and eternally generative.
