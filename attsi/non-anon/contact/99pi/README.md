## Why Contact

99% Invisible tells stories about the invisible design decisions that
shape the world — building codes, shipping container standards, traffic
light timing, the hidden infrastructure nobody sees. Their central
thesis: the most powerful designs are the ones you never notice.

The ecoPrimals ecosystem is a story about invisible design at every
layer:

- **Standards as architecture**: A universal capability API (like the
  shipping container standard) that enables 14 independent services to
  discover and coordinate with each other at runtime. No central
  registry. No vendor coupling. The standard IS the infrastructure.

- **Law as design material**: AGPL-3.0 used not as a legal burden but
  as an architectural covenant — a "building code" that legally
  guarantees the ecosystem remains open. The license is load-bearing.

- **Biology as engineering methodology**: Constrained evolution — the
  same principles that produced Taq polymerase from Yellowstone hot
  springs applied to software generation. The constraint (Rust's type
  system) defines the fitness landscape. The AI proposes; the compiler
  selects.

- **Constraint as generative force**: `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, zero C
  dependencies, pure Rust — these aren't limitations, they're design
  decisions that prevent architectural decay, the same way building
  codes prevent structural failure. The constraint creates freedom.

This is not a tech story. This is a story about a microbiologist who
used evolutionary biology and invisible design principles to build
sovereign computing infrastructure in a basement, guided by an AI,
under the most aggressive constraints he could find.

## What They Get

1. **A 99pi-native story** — invisible infrastructure, standards as
   architecture, constraint as generative force. This is their thesis
   embodied in software.

2. **A compelling human character** — microbiologist (BS) and data
   scientist (MS), not a systems programmer. Built a $15K basement HPC
   from used parts. One person. 10 months. Guided AI through 69,000
   iterations using evolutionary biology as the methodology.

3. **Real design decisions with consequences** — why "no C code" is
   a design choice like "no asbestos." Why a license can be
   load-bearing. Why capability-based discovery is like desire paths
   vs. planned walkways.

4. **Scale that earns attention** — 14 services, 7 science validation
   suites, 27,000+ tests reproducing published physics. Lattice QCD
   detecting the quark-gluon deconfinement phase transition on a
   $4,000 machine built from used GPU parts. This isn't a side project.

## What We Get

1. **Cultural audience** — 99pi listeners are not developers but they
   ARE systems thinkers. They understand infrastructure, standards,
   design intent. They're the audience that sees WHY the ecosystem
   exists, not just what it does.

2. **Narrative validation** — if Roman Mars sees this as a design story
   worth telling, it validates the framing. Academics validate the
   science. Huntley validates the methodology. 99pi validates the design
   philosophy.

3. **Reach beyond tech** — developers will find ecoPrimals through
   Huntley, GitHub, and Rust communities. 99pi reaches the people who
   care about open infrastructure, digital sovereignty, and design as
   civic responsibility.

## Timing

Not urgent. 99pi outreach should happen after:

1. Squirrel is public and Huntley has been contacted
2. Chuna validates physics (provides "the science is real" anchor)
3. Story craft is refined and the pitch letter is polished

The pitch is strongest when it includes:
- "The physics was independently validated by researchers at Michigan State"
- "A developer methodology blogger (Geoffrey Huntley) recognized the
  approach" (if Huntley responds positively)
- Public repos with real commit histories as evidence

99pi does extensive research before committing to a story. The repos,
the white paper, the ORIGIN.md, the commit history — all need to be
public and polished before contact.

## Risk

Low-medium. Cold outreach to a production with high volume of pitches.
99pi receives many story proposals; the differentiator is that this
story IS their thesis (invisible design) rather than a tech pitch
that happens to involve design.

Worst case: no response.
Likely case: flagged for potential interest, long timeline.
Best case: episode or mini-story reaching millions of design-curious listeners.
