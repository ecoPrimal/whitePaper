## The Offer

Your lab has data. We have a validated computational pipeline that runs
on existing hardware at zero cost. You send us data, we send back
predictions and testable hypotheses. You test them at the bench.

No software to install. No licenses. No cloud bills. No FTE.
No learning curve. You keep working exactly how you work now.

## Why It's Free

The pipeline runs on consumer hardware already in the lab. No
recurring costs:

| Component | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Software (all springs) | Open source, AGPL-3.0 |
| GPU compute (biomeGate) | Already built, in-lab |
| HPC compute (ICER) | Existing MSU allocation |
| Data sources (public) | GEO, PDB, ChEMBL, GTEx, etc. |
| Personnel | Already funded (RA III position) |

Commercial equivalents (NONMEM, Monolix, WinNonlin, Galaxy, CRO
modeling) cost $75K–210K per pipeline per year. This costs electricity.

## What We Need From You

1. **Data** — whatever format you already export. CSV, FASTQ, Excel.
   We'll handle the conversion.
2. **Expertise** — tell us what the results mean in your biological
   system. We know the math; you know the biology.
3. **Bench validation** — test the predictions we generate. That's
   the experiment that matters.

---

## What You Get Back

- **Preliminary data for grants** — computational results that support
  R01/R21 aims. Reviewers see validated predictions before you spend
  reagent money.
- **New hypotheses** — patterns in your existing data you haven't
  looked at. Compounds classified "inactive" that may be functional
  low-affinity binders. Cross-species predictions from tissue geometry.
- **Co-investigator relationships** — joint publications, shared
  preliminary data, stronger grant proposals.
- **Reanalysis of old screens** — every dose-response dataset your
  lab has ever collected can be reanalyzed through the binding
  landscape framework at no cost.
