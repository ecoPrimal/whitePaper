## Community Profile

ABG is a brand-new bioinformatics research Discord that grew from ~0 to 30+
admitted members in its first day. The rapid growth suggests high latent
demand for collaborative bioinformatics communities among early-career
researchers. The community skews toward:

- **Career stage**: Predominantly students and early postdocs (undergrad
  through final-year PhD), with a few senior members (assistant professor,
  university professor, neurosurgery resident)
- **Toolchain**: Python/R dominated. Standard bioinformatics stack: QIIME2,
  Snakemake, Nextflow, Galaxy, WDL. HPC for compute.
- **Domains**: NGS analysis, scRNA-seq, ATAC-seq, bulk RNA-seq, metagenomics,
  structural biology, drug discovery, medical imaging
- **Geography**: International — Canada, USA (EDT), Mexico, Australia
  (Sydney), Europe
- **Culture**: Open, eager to learn, collaborative. Several members
  volunteered to moderate. Low gatekeeping.

## SEER Analysis

SEER's pitch maps directly to problems that ecoPrimals has already solved
with validated, open-source tools:

| SEER Claim | ecoPrimals Equivalent | Status |
|-----------|----------------------|--------|
| "Detecting subtle regime shifts" | Anderson localization framework for microbial QS regime classification | Validated: 299K QS genes, 170 metagenomes, 34 QS types (wetSpring Exp144-145) |
| "Early risk patterns" | ESN reservoir computing for bloom/contamination detection | Validated: AKD1000 NPU at 18.8K Hz (wetSpring Exp118, 123, 194) |
| "Hidden structure in complex time-series" | Spectral methods, batched eigensolve, Anderson spectral classification | Validated: hotSpring GPU shaders, groundSpring uncertainty budgets |
| "Compact enough for efficient deployment" | AKD1000 NPU: 10 mW, coin-cell battery, 11-year standby at 1 Hz | Validated: rustChip sovereign driver, live hardware |

SEER frames these as future capabilities to "explore." ecoPrimals has them
validated with thousands of checks.

**The pattern**: commercial entities join open research communities to learn
which problems researchers face, then build proprietary solutions. This is
not malicious — it's rational business — but it extracts value from the
community without returning it.

**The inversion**: publish community work under AGPL3 (or scyBorg triple:
AGPL-3.0-or-later + ORC + CC-BY-SA 4.0). Anyone who builds on the work
must share modifications back. Extraction becomes forced contribution.

## Tool Ecosystem Gap

ABG members use standard bioinformatics tools that ecoPrimals has sovereign
Rust equivalents for:

| Standard Tool | Role | ecoPrimals Equivalent | Advantage |
|--------------|------|----------------------|-----------|
| QIIME2 | 16S pipeline | wetSpring sovereign 16S (barraCuda) | No Python, no cloud, 6,656 checks |
| Snakemake/Nextflow/WDL | Workflow orchestration | biomeOS deploy graphs (TOML DAGs) | Native Rust, no JVM, capability routing |
| AlphaFold (Google API) | Structure prediction | helixVision (coralForge primitives) | Sovereign, $0.0001/prediction, no data sent to Google |
| Guppy/Dorado | Nanopore basecalling | barraCuda basecalling (planned) | Sovereign Rust, no Python runtime |
| Galaxy | Web-based analysis platform | petalTongue + biomeOS | Sovereign, runs on own hardware |
| HPC clusters | Compute | toadStool + barraCuda + coralReef | Consumer GPU, Vulkan, no CUDA, no allocation queue |
| readfish | Adaptive sampling | NPU adaptive sampling (AKD1000) | Sub-ms latency, 37x headroom, 10 mW |

Most ABG members are students who cannot afford HPC allocation. Consumer
GPU compute through Vulkan — "you already have the GPU in your gaming
PC" — directly addresses their resource constraint.
