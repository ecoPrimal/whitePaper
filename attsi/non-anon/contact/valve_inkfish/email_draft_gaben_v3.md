## Why v3 replaces v2

v2 was still framed as an ask — "give me a berth." That's wrong.

Kevin can process Inkfish genomic data from Michigan. He doesn't
need to be on a vessel. The value proposition works at a distance.

But he CAN work on a vessel — wet lab skills, PADI scuba cert,
military austere-environment experience. That's a bonus, not the
pitch.

The real pitch is: "I exist. Here's what I built. The overlap is
real. I'm available."

### The identity reveal

This is the only email where ecoPrimal drops the pseudonym. Every
other contact (99pi, Karpathy, GPU manufacturers, PIs) gets
ecoPrimal. Gabe gets Kevin Mok.

Why:
- Gabe is the person most likely to understand and respect the
  pseudonymous approach (Valve's flat structure, Steam's anonymous-
  by-default culture)
- The identity reveal IS the trust signal — it says "I trust you
  with this"
- It makes the email personal in a way no other outreach is
- It connects the Orange Box teenager to the 30-something
  microbiologist to the sovereign compute builder — one human arc

### What we removed

- "What I'm asking for" — removed entirely. Not asking.
- "Patron/sponsor arrangement" — too transactional
- "Berth on an expedition" — too specific. Let Gabe imagine the fit.
- Cost comparisons — too salesy
- CC to Pim — this email is personal. Pim gets his own email later
  if Gabe engages.

### What we kept

- "It runs on a Steam Deck" — the bridge sentence
- "I see the organisms that live inside the organisms" — the
  microbiology pitch
- The sovereignty parallel — the philosophical alignment

### Steam identity is structural, not a P.S.

The Steam ID and PC-building history open the email because they're
the reason the email exists. Kevin isn't a stranger cold-emailing
a billionaire. He's a long-term Steam user who builds his own PCs,
became a microbiologist, built sovereign compute on the GPU substrate
Valve championed, and realized the person funding the marine science
he wants to contribute to is the same person whose infrastructure
he's been using since he was a teenager. The Orange Box → army →
microbiology → Sandia → sovereign compute → Inkfish is one arc.

### PADI certification

In the bio paragraph. For Inkfish, PADI cert is baseline — their
dive team uses closed-circuit rebreathers. But it shows Kevin isn't
a pure desk scientist. He's been underwater.

### Tone

This reads like a letter from someone who's been in Gabe's ecosystem
for two decades, not a stranger pitching from outside. The email
wants nothing except to say: "I exist, the overlap is real, I'm
here if useful."
