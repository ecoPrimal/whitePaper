## I. West, Up the Mountain

A kid from Michigan starts college at 18. Organic chemistry, calculus,
Spanish. AP Computer Science already banked. Microbiology declared.

Then he goes west.

Schofield Barracks, Hawaii. 25th Infantry Division. Three years carrying
the weight. Not a metaphor — infantry carries weight. Rucksack, rifle,
radio. The RTO carries the most: $2 million in communications equipment,
zero loss. The COIST analyst carries something heavier: other people's
intelligence, consolidated for higher command. You debrief 130 troops.
You build terrain models from topographical maps. You install
cryptographic keys for secure telecommunications.

You are 21 years old and you are already a membrane — the surface
between information and the command that needs it. You don't generate
the data. You don't make the decisions. You are the routing layer.
The thin surface. The Caddy reverse proxy of a combat unit.

That's Atlas. The Titan who holds up the sky. Not because he wants to.
Because someone has to.

Prokaryotes. The 3.8 billion year old survivors. The original membrane.

## VI. Atlas Becomes Talos

Atlas holds up the sky. He can't put it down. The weight is the
function. Remove the weight, remove the purpose. Atlas IS the
carrying.

Talos walks the perimeter. Three times daily, around Crete. Bronze
body. Ichor for blood. One vein, ankle to neck. When invaders approach,
Talos throws rocks. When they pierce his ankle, the ichor leaks out
and he falls.

The difference:
- Atlas is **static**. He holds. He endures. He carries.
- Talos is **dynamic**. He patrols. He detects. He responds.
- Atlas is the painted plate (Paper 98).
- Talos is the self-spinning plate.

The ecoPrimal sovereign mesh is Talos:

Kevin Mok went from carrying the sky (infantry, bioreactors, PFAS)
