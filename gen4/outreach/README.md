## Tone Principle

gen3 outreach (attsi/ briefs) speaks to faculty, committees, and
technical evaluators. gen4 outreach speaks to **people who want to
make things.** The tone is practical, the jargon is minimal, and the
first question answered is always: "what can I do with this?"

---

*Each audience enters from a different door. Each door leads to the
same infrastructure.*
