## Tone Principle

gen3 outreach (attsi/ briefs) speaks to faculty, committees, and
technical evaluators. gen4 outreach speaks to **people who want to
make things, do science, or support the commons.** The tone is
practical, the jargon is minimal, and the first question answered
is always: "what can I do with this?"

---

## The Economics Connection

Every outreach document connects to the gen4 economic model:

- **Creatives** earn through sunCloud attribution when their content
  generates value (content packs, NFTs, lending)
- **Builders** compose primals into sporeGarden products that
  participate in the metabolic mandate
- **Researchers** contribute sediment layers to the foundation;
  their work is the flywheel's first turn
- **Supporters** fund the flywheel directly — money, hardware, or
  sweat equity, all permanently attributed via Loam Certificates

See `gen4/economics/THE_FLYWHEEL.md` for the seed-stage economics and
`gen4/economics/SUNCLOUD_ACTIVATION.md` for the product-stage model.

## Sovereign Allies

The ecosystem identifies **sovereign allies** — organizations whose
structural incentives align with open science and whose platforms
resist capture by external commercial or political pressure:

| Ally | Why Sovereign |
|------|--------------|
| **Valve / Steam** | Privately held (no shareholder pressure). Invested in Linux, Proton, Vulkan, SteamOS. Won't bend to Microsoft, Apple, or platform aggregators. 132M users with GPUs. Gabe exploring the ocean — our tech applies to both gaming and deep-sea science. |
| **System76** | Pure Rust firmware (Pop!_OS, coreboot). Open hardware. AGPL-aligned philosophy. Potential for pre-configured sovereign research nodes. |
| **BrainChip** | Neuromorphic NPU (AKD1000). 10 mW inference. Enables field science without cloud. |
| **NCBI / NIH** | Public data mandate. Open access. Provenance chain anchors — the bedrock of the foundation. |

Sovereign allies are not business partners. They are organizations you
can rely on — structurally, not contractually. Valve won't revoke your
access to Proton because an investor got nervous. NCBI won't put
genomes behind a paywall because a quarterly report disappointed.

The gen3 invitations (outreach/AN_INVITATION_TO_VALVE_V2.md,
outreach/A_LETTER_TO_GABE_NEWELL.md, outreach/AN_INVITATION_TO_NVIDIA_V3.md)
present the technical case. gen4 outreach presents the human case:
here's what you can do, here's what it costs, here's who's doing it.
