## 1. The Argument

gen3 asked "does it work?" and proved yes — 12,510+ quantitative checks
across 70+ reproduced papers in 8 scientific domains, produced in ~69
days by 8 springs, 14 primals, and a constrained evolution methodology
formalized in a PhD thesis.

gen4 asks "who uses it?" and answers with products (esotericWebb,
helixVision, blueFish) that compose primals into tools where the
infrastructure is invisible.

But between the question and the answer is the **foundation** — the
complete validated scientific lineage that gives products their
substance. Without the foundation, products are empty containers.
Without products, the foundation is an archive. Together they are a
living ecosystem: the foundation validates, products deploy, and the
cycle repeats as constraints evolve.

This document maps the foundation as a unified whole.

### Why Unified?

The 28 baseCamp papers are not 28 disconnected studies. Anderson
localization (Paper 01) threads through microbial ecology (03, 04, 05,
06), immunology (12), anaerobic digestion (16), and plasma physics (07).
The provenance trio validates game sessions (18, 19), biological samples
(21), and medical records (22) using identical DAG/certificate/braid
machinery. barraCuda's GPU math powers lattice QCD (07, 10b, 25),
protein structure (10a), bloom detection (04), and drug screening (12).
healthSpring's kinetic models (13) share ODE solvers with whole-cell
metabolism (ABG Paper E) and plasma transport (07).

Separating these into independent foundations would sever the
connections that make the ecosystem powerful. The foundation is one
interconnected graph. Expression documents zoom into subgraphs.

### The Continued Outcome

The constraints did not stop evolving after gen3. Eight springs absorbed
new science domains. The library-to-binary rewiring matured through four
tiers. The provenance trio became operationally validated end-to-end.
External contact — ABG community, Murillo group, Gonzales lab,
Kachkovskiy's spectral theory, Liao's anaerobic digestion research —
exposed systems the ecosystem had not yet expressed.

This document is the continued outcome of that evolution. Every new
contact, every new paper, every new spring experiment adds a node to the
lineage graph. The foundation grows as the ecosystem grows.

## 6. The scyBorg Publication Strategy

The unified lineage is publishable as a single sovereign publication
under the scyBorg triple license:

- **AGPL-3.0-or-later**: All code (spring experiments, deploy graphs,
  NUCLEUS compositions). Derivatives share modifications back.
- **ORC**: System mechanics and composition patterns. Irrevocable.
- **CC-BY-SA 4.0**: This document and all expression documents.
  Share-alike ensures the knowledge stays open.

### What Gets Published

1. **This document** — The unified lineage map. A methods paper describing
   how one interconnected foundation feeds multiple products through
   NUCLEUS composition.

2. **Expression documents** — Deep dives into domain threads (ABG
   whole-cell rebuild is the first). Each publishable independently as a
   domain-specific reproduction study.

3. **The foundation itself** — Every spring experiment, every reproduced
   paper, every provenance-complete computation is a sovereign publication:
   DAG-recorded, BLAKE3-hashed, ed25519-witnessed, loamSpine-committed.

4. **Product outputs** — When helixVision processes a sample, the output
   carries the foundation's provenance chain. When blueFish converts a
   file, the conversion is content-addressed. The products publish the
   foundation's integrity with every use.

### The Anchoring Pipeline

Each published artifact follows the sovereign publication pipeline from
`gen4/thesis/THE_SOVEREIGN_PUBLICATION.md`:

```
Computation (NUCLEUS deploy graph)
  → DAG recording (rhizoCrypt)
  → Signing (BearDog ed25519)
  → Dehydration (loamSpine permanent ledger)
  → Attribution (sweetGrass braid)
  → Anchoring (BTC/ETH cryptographic timestamp)
  → Publication (NestGate content-addressed)
```
