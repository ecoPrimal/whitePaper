## How These Relate to gen3

gen3's thesis is formalized in `gen3/thesis/` and the `atlasHugged`
series. The core arguments — constrained evolution as methodology,
mobility edge as network theory, sovereignty as architecture — are
the foundations gen4 builds on.

gen4's thesis documents do not replace gen3's. They extend them into a
new domain: from "does the infrastructure compute correctly?" to "does
the infrastructure enable creative acts that couldn't exist without it?"

| gen3 thesis | gen4 extension |
|-------------|----------------|
| Constrained evolution | Creative constraints (bounded topology, authored endings) |
| Mobility edge | Creative surface as hopping term |
| Sovereignty as architecture | Invisible sovereignty (creator never sees the stack) |
| Filter is the net | Products are the net (usage replaces reproduction) |
