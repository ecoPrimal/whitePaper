## The Anti-Extraction Principle

Every economic document in gen4 shares a core principle: **value flows
along attribution braids, not to platforms.** The creator, the player,
the AI, and the infrastructure all get credit. No single entity
extracts disproportionate rent.

This is not altruism. It is architecture. Systems that extract too much
value from their participants lose those participants. Systems that
distribute value fairly retain and attract them. The metabolic mandate
is the minimum viable extraction — just enough to keep the
infrastructure alive, not enough to starve the creative surface.
