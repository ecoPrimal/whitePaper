## What sunCloud Is

It is not a cryptocurrency. It is not a payment processor. It is a

## The Metabolic Mandate

Every living system has a metabolic cost — energy required to maintain
structure. The ecoPrimals ecosystem is no different:

- Primals need hosting (compute, storage, bandwidth)
- Springs need maintenance (updates, reproductions, absorption)
- Products need development (features, content, bug fixes)
- The wateringHole needs curation (standards, glossary, guidance)

The metabolic mandate is the principle that **a percentage of all value
flow through the ecosystem is reserved for infrastructure maintenance.**
This is not a tax — it is a thermodynamic requirement. Systems that
don't feed their metabolism die.

### Metabolic Rate

The metabolic rate is configurable per niche but has ecosystem-level
bounds:

| Layer | Metabolic rate | Feeds |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Infrastructure (primals) | 3–7% | Compute hosting, binary deployment |
| Science (springs) | 2–5% | Reproduction maintenance, data hosting |
| Product (sporeGarden) | 0% | Products keep their own revenue |
| Ecosystem (wateringHole) | 1–3% | Standards curation, coordination |

The product layer has zero metabolic overhead — products keep everything
they earn minus the infrastructure and ecosystem contributions. This
is by design: the creative surface should not be taxed by the platform.
The platform sustains itself through the metabolic mandate on
infrastructure and science layers.

## Attribution Distribution

When value flows through the ecosystem, sweetGrass braids determine
where it goes:

### Example: A Player Buys a Content Pack

```
Player pays $5 for "Thornwood Tales" content pack
    │
    ├── Creator: $3.25 (65%)
    │   └── sweetGrass braid: solo_creator_handle
    │
    ├── Derived-from credits: $0.50 (10%)
    │   ├── Ruleset author: $0.25 (if using someone else's RulesetCert)
    │   └── Template author: $0.25 (if derived from a template world)
    │
    ├── Infrastructure metabolic: $0.50 (10%)
    │   ├── rhizoCrypt hosting: $0.15
    │   ├── Squirrel AI hosting: $0.20
    │   └── General primal infra: $0.15
    │
    ├── Science metabolic: $0.25 (5%)
    │   └── ludoSpring maintenance (game science models)
    │
    ├── Ecosystem metabolic: $0.10 (2%)
    │   └── wateringHole curation
    │
    └── Treasury reserve: $0.40 (8%)
        └── Ecosystem development fund
```

### Example: A Playthrough Generates an NFT

When a playthrough produces a Novel Ferment Transcript that someone
else values (lends, trades, exhibits), the value flows along the
fermentation attribution braid:

```
NFT "Miravel's Trust Arc" — valued at $2 by collector
    │
    ├── Player who traversed: $1.00 (50%)
    │   (their choices created the unique traversal)
    │
    ├── Creator who authored: $0.60 (30%)
    │   (they built the world, NPCs, and rules)

```

The distribution percentages are encoded in the RulesetCert. The
creator sets them when designing the world. The engine enforces them.
sweetGrass records them. There is no platform that can override the
creator's terms.

## Treasury

The treasury is the ecosystem's reserve fund. It accumulates from the
metabolic mandate and funds:

1. **Infrastructure development**: New primal features, performance
   improvements, security audits
2. **Science reproduction**: Maintaining springs, reproducing new papers,
   expanding baseCamp
3. **Ecosystem tooling**: wateringHole standards, Songbird discovery,
   biomeOS coordination
4. **Grants**: Funding creators who build on the ecosystem (especially
   early, before revenue flows)

### Treasury Governance

The treasury is not controlled by a single entity. Governance follows
the ecosystem's biological model:

- **Proposals** are sweetGrass-attributed documents
- **Voting** is weighted by ecosystem contribution (not capital)
- **Execution** is traceable via rhizoCrypt DAGs
- **Accountability** is enforced by public loamSpine records

This is designed but not implemented. gen4 activates the economic
infrastructure; gen5 (or late gen4) activates the governance layer.

## The Anti-Platform Principle

sunCloud is not a platform. Platforms extract value in exchange for
access. sunCloud distributes value along attribution braids without
gatekeeping access.

| Property | Platform model | sunCloud model |
|----------|---------------|----------------|
| Access | Gatekept (app store, marketplace) | Open (sovereign distribution) |
| Revenue | 30% platform cut (Apple, Steam, etc.) | 5–15% metabolic (configurable) |
| Attribution | Platform gets credit | All contributors get credit |
| Data | Platform owns | Creator and player own |
| Terms | Platform sets | Creator sets (in RulesetCert) |
| Portability | Platform-locked | Sovereign (export anytime) |
| Governance | Corporate | Ecosystem (contribution-weighted) |

The 30% cut that Apple and Steam take is a rent extraction mechanism.
The 5–15% metabolic rate that sunCloud collects is a maintenance
mechanism. The difference is architectural: rent flows to shareholders,
maintenance flows to infrastructure.

## Activation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Attribution Infrastructure (current)

sweetGrass braids are wired in esotericWebb V4. Every composition
pipeline run records attribution. The infrastructure for tracking "who
contributed what" exists.

### Phase 2: Value Quantification

Assign economic value to creative acts. This requires:
- Content pack pricing (creator sets price)
- NFT valuation (market-based, not platform-set)
- Subscription models (recurring access to evolving worlds)
- Patronage (direct creator support)

### Phase 3: Distribution Implementation

Build the actual payment routing along sweetGrass braids:
- BearDog-signed payment records
- loamSpine receipts (permanent, auditable)
- Multi-currency support (fiat, optional crypto, barter)
- Metabolic mandate automatic deduction

### Phase 4: Ecosystem Economics

Close the economic loop:
- Treasury accumulates from metabolic mandate
- Treasury funds infrastructure and grants
- Creators earn from content
- Players earn from NFTs
- Attribution flows everywhere
- The ecosystem sustains itself
