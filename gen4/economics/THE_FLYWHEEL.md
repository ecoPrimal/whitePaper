## The Gap

The gen4 economic model is coherent at product scale:

```
Products generate revenue
    → sunCloud distributes along sweetGrass braids
    → Metabolic mandate feeds infrastructure
    → Treasury funds grants and development
    → Ecosystem sustains itself
```

But products don't generate revenue yet. esotericWebb is in V4.
helixVision is emerging. blueFish is planned. The gap between "now"
and "sunCloud distributes" is real:

```
Now:    One person pays electricity, hardware, time. Nobody pays them.
Future: Products generate revenue, sunCloud distributes it.
Gap:    The flywheel.
```

The flywheel fills the gap. It is not a detour from the economic model
— it is the first expression of it.

## The Flywheel Loop

```
You offer compute / infrastructure / expertise
    ↓
Community produces science on it (ABG, springs, foundation)
    ↓
Science proves the ecosystem works (publications, validated results)
    ↓
People see value and contribute (money, hardware, time, science)
    ↓
Contributions grow the infrastructure (more nodes, more storage)
    ↓
More infrastructure → more science → more proof → more contributions
    ↓
(repeat — each turn adds mass)
```

This IS sunCloud at seed scale. The "product" is the ecosystem itself.
The "customers" are supporters who see value in open sovereign science.
The attribution braids are the same — sweetGrass records who
contributed what.

## What the Flywheel Accepts

### Money

Direct support for metabolic costs:

| Amount | What It Covers |
|--------|---------------|
| $10/month | Electricity for one gate for ~2 weeks |
| $50/month | Covers full metabolic cost of dedicated ABG node |
| $200 | 1 TB NVMe drive for fast scratch storage |
| $500 | 4 TB HDD for cold dataset storage |

Mechanisms: GitHub Sponsors, Open Collective, Liberapay, direct
transfer — the channel matters less than the transparency. Every
dollar is attributed via sweetGrass. Contributors see what their
support enabled.

### Hardware

Physical contributions to the mesh:

| Contribution | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| Spare GPU (3060, 3070, 3090, etc.) | Adds VRAM to the work card pool |
| Old tower (any generation) | Becomes a new gate in the mesh |
| NVMe / HDD | Adds storage where workloads need it |
| Networking gear (10G NIC, switch) | Expands backbone bandwidth |
| NPU (Akida, Coral, etc.) | Adds neuromorphic substrate |

Hardware donations are registered in NestGate and attributed in
sweetGrass. The donor's name is in the provenance braid of every
computation that hardware performs. A donated 3090 that powers 47
ABG validation runs carries the donor's attribution in 47 sediment
layers. That's not a tax receipt — it's a geological record.

### Sweat Equity

Time and expertise:

| Contribution | Attribution |
|-------------|-------------|
| Science (ABG member validates a paper) | sweetGrass braid: researcher attribution |
| Code (contributor writes a spring binary) | AGPL attribution + sweetGrass |
| Documentation (someone improves wateringHole) | CC-BY-SA attribution + sweetGrass |
| Outreach (someone connects a faculty member) | sweetGrass: bridge attribution |
| Sysadmin (someone helps configure a gate) | sweetGrass: infrastructure attribution |

Sweat equity is tracked identically to monetary and hardware
contributions. The braids don't distinguish between $50 and 50 hours.
Both are contributions. Both propagate.

## Loam Certificates for Contributions

Every contribution — money, hardware, time — gets a Loam Certificate.
The certificate records:

```
Certificate: Contribution
  ├── contributor: identity (BearDog-verified or pseudonymous)
  ├── type: monetary | hardware | sweat_equity
  ├── value: $50 | "RTX 3070 (8 GB GDDR6)" | "16S pipeline validation"
  ├── date: 2026-05-07
  ├── enabled:
  │   ├── gate: ironGate (NVMe upgrade)
  │   └── workloads: 12 ABG validation runs
  ├── attribution_braid: sweetgrass_braid_id
  └── provenance: loamSpine chain hash
```

The certificate ferments. As the hardware powers more science and the
money enables more infrastructure, the certificate's `enabled` section
grows. A $200 NVMe donation's certificate might eventually read:

```
enabled:
  ├── 47 ABG validation runs
  ├── 3 publications under scyBorg
  ├── 12,000 BLAKE3-hashed datasets
  └── foundation sediment layers 12–58
```

The contribution's value compounds through the geological record.

## The Metabolic Cost

The ecosystem's thermodynamic minimum — what it costs to stay alive:

| Cost | Monthly | Annual |
|------|---------|--------|
| Electricity (11 nodes) | ~$150 | ~$1,800 |
| Internet (JupyterHub, data fetching) | ~$80 | ~$960 |
| Domain / DNS | ~$5 | ~$60 |
| Hardware depreciation (~$15K / 5yr) | ~$250 | ~$3,000 |
| **Total metabolic** | **~$485** | **~$5,820** |

This is the metabolic mandate at its most literal. The ecosystem's
thermodynamic cost of existing. If 50 people contribute $10/month,
the metabolism is covered. If 10 people contribute $50/month, same
result. The bar is low because the architecture is sovereign — no
cloud bills, no subscription fees, no platform cuts.

Your time is not priced here. That's the sweat equity. The metabolic
cost is the cash floor — what must be paid in currency to keep
electrons flowing.

## The Bidirectional Vision

### Phase 1: Inward (now)

Supporters contribute money and hardware. The mesh grows. ABG gets
compute. Science gets produced. The flywheel turns.

### Phase 2: Outward (months to year)

The mesh generates surplus capacity. A configured gate — a recycled
tower, a NUC, whatever fits the need — gets NUCLEUS pre-deployed and
shipped to a researcher who needs it.

An ABG member at a university in a resource-constrained setting
receives a $500–1,000 piece of hardware that gives them $50K+ in
equivalent operational compute value. Their work flows back as sediment
layers in the foundation. sweetGrass connects the hardware donor
(Michigan) → you (ironGate) → the researcher (wherever they are) →
the science (NCBI → validation → publication). Radiating attribution
across geography.

### Phase 3: Distributed (ongoing)

Enough outward gates exist that the mesh becomes geographically
distributed. Songbird federation connects them. Plasmodium bonding
coordinates workloads across gates. The flywheel is no longer one
person offering compute — it's a network of sovereign gates, each
contributing and consuming, each depositing sediment.

This is the gen2 vision realized:

> As the network grows, the value term for each participant increases,
> while their individual cost to achieve a given outcome decreases.
> This creates a powerful, self-reinforcing incentive loop.
>
> — gen2/EcoPrimals_Whitepaper_Draft §4.5

## What the Flywheel Is Not

**Not a startup.** There is no equity, no cap table, no exit strategy.
The ecosystem is AGPL/ORC/CC-BY-SA. It cannot be acquired because
there is nothing to acquire.

**Not a charity.** Contributors get structural attribution, not a
thank-you email. Their names are in the geological record of every
computation their contribution enabled. The attribution compounds.

**Not a cryptocurrency.** There is no token, no coin, no speculative
asset. Money is money. Hardware is hardware. Time is time. sunCloud
will eventually route value along braids, but that value is real
(fiat, barter, reciprocal compute) — not a manufactured asset.

**Not a platform.** There is no marketplace, no app store, no 30% cut.
The metabolic mandate (3–15%) feeds infrastructure, not shareholders.
The flywheel turns because people see value, not because they're
locked in.

## The Connection to sunCloud

The flywheel and sunCloud are not separate systems. They are the same
system at different scales:

| | Flywheel (seed stage) | sunCloud (product stage) |
|---|---|---|
| **Value source** | Donations, hardware, sweat equity | Product revenue (games, tools, science) |
| **Distribution** | Direct to metabolic costs + hardware | sweetGrass braids → attributed splits |
| **Attribution** | Loam Certificates for contributions | Loam Certificates for all value flow |
| **Metabolic** | 100% goes to survival | 5–15% metabolic, rest to creators |
| **Treasury** | Not yet (all funds = metabolic) | Accumulates from metabolic mandate |
| **Governance** | Architect decides (bootstrapping) | Contribution-weighted ecosystem |

As products launch and generate revenue, the flywheel's donor-funded
metabolism transitions to sunCloud's product-funded metabolism. The
attribution infrastructure is identical — sweetGrass braids from day
one. The certificates are the same. The geological record is
continuous.

Early contributors don't get diluted. They get compounded. Their
braids are in the earliest sediment layers — the deepest geology.
When the ecosystem is mature, the bedrock still carries their names.
